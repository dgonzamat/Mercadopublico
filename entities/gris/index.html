Grey

Head large in proportion to the body, dark almond-shaped eyes, pale or waxy skin. The corpus's dominant morphotype.

Socorro (Zamora)· 1964

On 24 April 1964, police officer Lonnie Zamora broke off a chase to investigate a white egg-shaped object resting on legs, with two small figures nearby, outside Socorro, New Mexico. Project Blue Book classified it "Unknown", with landing marks and scorched vegetation verified on site by Hynek, who cited it as the case that changed his stance.

Ariel School· 1994

On 16 September 1994, 62 children at the Ariel School (Ruwa, Zimbabwe) watched a silver object settle beside the playground and small humanoid figures; Dr. John Mack of Harvard Medical School interviewed them with formal psychiatric methodology, and their accounts stayed consistent for three decades. The best-documented child-witness case in the corpus.

1994S77%152🇺🇸United States.

Barney and Betty Hill· 1961

On the night of 19 September 1961, Betty and Barney Hill reported being followed by a light near Lancaster, New Hampshire, and, under later hypnosis with psychiatrist Benjamin Simon, recounted an abduction. Pease AFB logged a correlated radar return in Project Blue Book; Simon himself judged the memories to be fantasy, not recall.

1961A40%153🇺🇸United States.

Travis Walton (Snowflake)· 1975

On 5 November 1975, Travis Walton and six fellow members of a forestry crew in Arizona saw a glowing disc-shaped object; by the convergent testimony of all six, Walton approached it and vanished for five days. The seven reported the event to the sheriff before the outcome was known, five passed polygraphs, and an institutional search by the Forest Service and USAF is documented.

1975A50%26🇷🇺USSR / Russia.

Voronezh· 1989

After children and adults reported an object and humanoid figures in a Voronezh park, the official Soviet agency TASS published a Glasnost-era statement calling the visit a 'scientifically proven fact' — unparalleled in official superpower communications.

1989A32%175🇧🇷Brazil.

Antônio Vilas-Boas· 1957

On the night of 16 October 1957, 23-year-old farmer Antônio Vilas-Boas said he was forcibly taken from his tractor by short figures and brought inside a landed object in São Francisco de Sales, where he was examined and subjected to a reproductive-type interaction. What sets the case apart from a mere tale is that physician Olavo Fontes examined him months later and documented skin lesions and symptoms consistent with radiation exposure. It is, chronologically, the first documented modern abduction —four years before Betty and Barney Hill— and a foundational landmark of all subsequent casework.

FBI memos · humanoid 'crewmen' (PURSUE)· 1965

FBI memos from the 1960s, declassified in the PURSUE release (2026), that log witness reports of small humanoid 'crewmen' —wearing suits and helmets— emerging from unidentified craft. They belong to the occupant-encounter category (CE3): low evidential value (testimonial, no physical corroboration), included for documentary completeness and as a sample of the kind of material the FBI archived.

Herbert Schirmer · the Ashland patrolman· 1967

In the early hours of December 3, 1967, 22-year-old police officer Herbert Schirmer reported an oval object hovering over Highway 6 near Ashland, Nebraska, and immediately noticed a red mark under his ear and unaccounted-for minutes on his shift. The case reached the University of Colorado's Condon Committee, which in 1968 had Schirmer hypnotized by psychologist Leo Sprinkle (University of Wyoming) and polygraphed — he passed. Under hypnosis he described pale grey-skinned occupants with slanted eyes —the grey archetype, not reptilian despite a winged-serpent emblem on their uniforms— and a warning of two future visits. The Condon Committee itself labeled the case 'psychological'.

1967B28%225🇫🇮Finland.

Imjärvi encounter · Finland· 1970

On January 7, 1970, two Finnish skiers —Aarno Heinonen and Esko Viljo— said they encountered in a snowy clearing near Imjärvi an object about 3 meters across wrapped in reddish mist, from which a small figure with a black box of pulsating light descended on a light beam. Heinonen was left as if paralyzed and, in the following days, suffered numbness, vomiting and dark urine. The case was investigated by Finnish ufologists and Flying Saucer Review; its credibility was eroded by the dozens of later sightings Heinonen claimed. Tier B: two witnesses and physical effects, testimonial anchor.

1970B40%160🇯🇵Japan.

Kōfu Incident · occupant encounter and physical traces (Japan, 1975)· 1975

On February 23, 1975, two schoolboys from Kōfu (Yamanashi prefecture, Japan), Masato Kawano and Katsuhiro Yamahata, said they saw two orange objects descend, one of which landed in a vineyard; from it a short occupant allegedly emerged and touched one boy on the shoulder, leaving him momentarily paralyzed. The case is unusual among ground sightings for its corroboration: besides the boys, it was reported by their mothers, a classmate, a janitor and a woman driving by, and two concrete posts were left toppled at the site, which school staff concluded the children could not have knocked over; a teacher measured anomalous radioactivity in the soil with a Geiger counter. The official explanation —the Civil Aviation Bureau attributed it to the lights of a YS-11 aircraft— covers the aerial lights but not the landing or the occupant. A classic Japanese case, told with its counter-explanation.

1975B25%214🇺🇸United States.

The Allagash Abductions· 1976

In August 1976, four art students —twins Jack and Jim Weiner, Charlie Foltz and Chuck Rak— were camping at Eagle Lake, in Maine's Allagash wilderness. They reported a large luminous sphere, a beam of light and a missing-time episode; years later, under fifteen hypnosis sessions, the four separately described an abduction and an examination. The case became an abduction classic — until in 2016 one of them, Chuck Rak, admitted the abduction part was made up.

1976B16%85🇺🇸United States.

Communion (Whitley Strieber)· 1985

On the night of 26 December 1985, writer Whitley Strieber recounted waking paralyzed in his upstate New York cabin and being led away by small beings with large black eyes. He told it in 'Communion' (1987), the bestseller that defined the modern image of the 'grey' and the abduction experience. Journalist Ed Conroy investigated the case. Skeptics attribute it to sleep paralysis / hypnagogia; there is no physical evidence.

1985B20%246🇺🇸United States.

Linda Napolitano · Brooklyn Bridge· 1989

In the early hours of 30 November 1989, Linda Napolitano claimed she was lifted by a beam of light from the 12th floor of her Lower East Side apartment in Manhattan and taken into a craft near the Brooklyn Bridge by three grey beings. Investigator Budd Hopkins made it his flagship case in 'Witnessed' (1997), citing more than 20 anonymous witnesses.

1989B22%162🇮🇱Israel.

Kadima · encounter wave with traces and police response (Israel, 1993)· 1993

Between March and June 1993, in the moshav of Kadima (near Netanya, Israel), several people —mostly women, in independent accounts— claimed encounters with a tall, bald 'gray'-type entity. The episode left verifiable material elements: circular depressions in a field with sampled residues (gelatinous material, reddish liquid, silicon deposits) and deep footprints that police tracked for several kilometers. The institutional response was real —about 30 Border Police officers combed the area— but the case's diffusion was dominated by sensational ufology (Barry Chamish), which erodes its credibility. It is the best-documented Israeli case, and it illustrates a high-strangeness point with fragile evidence: it enters the corpus as a Middle East opener and contrast case, not as an evidential anchor.
