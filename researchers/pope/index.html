Biography

British Ministry of Defence civil servant who from 1991 to 1994 held the 'UFO desk' (Secretariat Air Staff 2A), investigating reports of aerial phenomena for their possible impact on national defence. After leaving the post he made his conclusions public —his time inside the official apparatus— in 'Open Skies, Closed Minds' (1996), becoming a media voice on the topic until his death in 2026.

Rendlesham Forest· 1980

Between 26 and 28 December 1980, USAF personnel at the Bentwaters and Woodbridge bases reported lights and a triangular object with engraved markings in Rendlesham Forest, next to a nuclear-weapons store. Deputy commander Charles Halt recorded the second night live and signed an official memo; ground marks and elevated radiation remained.

Cosford Incident· 1993

On the night of 30–31 March 1993, a wave of sightings crossed the UK with more than a hundred witnesses, including police and military personnel. At RAF Cosford a guard saw lights over the base; at RAF Shawbury a meteorological officer described a large object sweeping the field with a beam of light at low speed before accelerating away at high speed. Nick Pope, of the MoD 'UFO desk', investigated and rated it of 'considerable defence significance'. Much of the wave coincides with the re-entry of a Russian rocket booster that night; the close-range Shawbury sighting remained the disputed residue.
