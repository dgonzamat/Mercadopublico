Meng Zhaoguo

Logger at the Red Flag camp, Wuchang (Heilongjiang) · the best-documented contact case in China (1994)

Biography

He climbed Phoenix Mountain believing he was investigating a downed helicopter and afterward described being struck and, that same night, having an intimate encounter with a tall female figure. The case is exceptional for its provenance: 1990s Chinese ufology operated in a circuit almost disconnected from the Western one, so the account is not easily explained as a copy of the American template, and yet it reproduces several of its elements. He was examined by Chinese researchers and given a polygraph. The skin marks he displayed admit mundane explanations, and no instrumental verification supports the core of the account.

Meng Zhaoguo · Heilongjiang· 1994

In June 1994, lumberjack Meng Zhaoguo, of the Red Flag camp in Wuchang (Heilongjiang, China), saw a metallic shine on Mount Phoenix and went to investigate what he thought was a downed helicopter; he said he was struck and, that night, had an intimate encounter with a very tall woman. It is China's most famous contact case, examined by local UFO societies.
