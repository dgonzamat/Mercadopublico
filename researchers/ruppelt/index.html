Edward J. Ruppelt

USAF Captain + Director of Project Blue Book (1951–1953) + coined the term 'Unidentified Flying Object'

St. Louis Post-Dispatch, 8 mar 1953 (recorte de la foto en en.wikipedia) · Public domain in the US (PD-US)

Biography

The first professional director of the US Air Force program to investigate the phenomenon (Project Blue Book). His 1956 book is the first memoir written from inside that apparatus, and he defended a rigorous methodology against pressure from above to label everything 'identified'. He died of a heart attack at 37, one of the cases in pattern 8c, the premature death of critical witnesses.

Twining Memo

A SECRET classified letter dated 23 September 1947, titled 'AMC Opinion Concerning "Flying Discs"', signed by Lieutenant General Nathan F. Twining, commander of the Air Materiel Command, and addressed to General Schulgen. It states that 'the phenomenon reported is something real and not visionary or fictitious', concedes that some incidents may be natural phenomena such as meteors, and recommends a priority, code-named study, which crystallized into Project Sign. Its limit is its basis: it is the opinion of engineers and analysts on witness interrogation reports, not a field investigation of its own, and it notes the lack of recovered crash debris. The text was published in 1969 in Appendix R of the Condon Report.

Project Blue Book — USAF (1952–1969)

Project Blue Book was the official USAF program between March 1952 and December 1969, successor to Sign and Grudge, run from the Air Technical Intelligence Center at Wright-Patterson. According to the USAF fact sheet, it received 12,618 sightings between 1947 and 1969, of which 701 (5.6%) remained 'Unidentified'. Its closure, announced on 17 December 1969, rested on the Condon Report and its review by the National Academy of Sciences. The Bolender memorandum (20 October 1969) noted that reports affecting national security went through other channels and 'are not part of the Blue Book system'. The files have been at the National Archives since 1976 (Record Group 341).
