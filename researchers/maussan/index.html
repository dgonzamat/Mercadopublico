Jaime Maussan

Mexican television journalist · host of the program "Tercer Milenio"

Dimaussan, 2015 (Wikimedia Commons) · CC-BY-SA 4.0

Biography

José Jaime Maussan Flota started out as a television reporter in the 1970s, on Jacobo Zabludovsky's team, and since the early 1990s has worked mainly on UFOs. In 1991 he aired the video Guillermo Arreola shot during the eclipse in Mexico City, which drew thousands of calls and new recordings; in 2004 he released, with the Mexican Defense Ministry's permission, the Campeche infrared video; and in 2023 he presented two mummified bodies to the Chamber of Deputies, saying they had been found in Nazca, Peru, and were alien to terrestrial evolution. What he presented faces documented objections: Robert Sheaffer and pilot Alejandro Franz attribute the Campeche lights to the flares of the Cantarell oil platforms; the National Autonomous University of Mexico (UNAM) reissued its 2017 statement that it had only carbon-dated the sample it received, without concluding anything about its origin, and in 2017 Peruvian prosecutors had described similar mummies as recently made dolls.
