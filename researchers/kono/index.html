Taro Kono

Minister of Defense of Japan (2019-2020) · Foreign Minister (2017-2019) · figure of the Liberal Democratic Party (LDP)

Gobierno de Japón (Kantei) · CC-BY 4.0

Biography

Japanese LDP politician, known for his direct style and international presence. As Minister of Defense, in September 2020 —days after the U.S. Navy officially released the FLIR videos— he ordered the Air Self-Defense Force to standardize the recording and reporting of unidentified aerial phenomena, which took shape in the Ministry of Defense's formal directive of September 14, 2020. It is Japan's first institutional step toward a UAP protocol, taken from an airspace-security logic —not from belief: Kono himself declared he was skeptical about UFOs, but judged that the chain of command should be prepared.

MoD UAP Reporting Protocol · Japan· 2020
