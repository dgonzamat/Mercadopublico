Biography

Representative for Missouri's 7th district since 2023 and a member of the House Oversight subcommittee that held the July 2023 UAP hearing with Grusch, Graves and Fravor. He argues the federal government is 'actively blocking' information on the phenomenon and insists pilots must be able to report without fear of reprisal. He is part of the bipartisan push for a standing congressional committee dedicated to UAP.
