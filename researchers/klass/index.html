Philip J. Klass

Electrical engineer · senior avionics editor at Aviation Week & Space Technology (1952–1986) · co-founder of CSICOP (1976)

Robert Sheaffer, congreso del CSICOP en Buffalo, 1983 (Wikimedia Commons) · CC-BY-SA 3.0

Biography

An electrical engineer, he worked for General Electric and from 1952 at Aviation Week & Space Technology magazine, where he was avionics editor. He began investigating UFO cases in 1966 and continued until 2003. His first book (1968) attributed many sightings to plasmas similar to ball lightning, an idea he later dropped; from then on he proposed causes case by case: ground radars and an airliner in the RB-47 case, Venus at St. Clair, a hoax at Socorro and Pascagoula, and fantasy in Betty Andreasson's hypnosis sessions. In 1976 he helped found CSICOP, the skeptics' committee that publishes Skeptical Inquirer magazine, and from 1989 to 2002 he put out the Skeptics UFO Newsletter. Some of his fraud accusations were never proved: in the case of deputy Val Johnson, he argued that Johnson himself had damaged his patrol car.
