James E. McDonald

Primary framework: Plurality (UAP Codex position)

PhD in physics, University of Iowa + Professor of Atmospheric Sciences, University of Arizona + 1,500+ pages of testimony to Congress, 1968

United States Government · Public domain · US Government work

Biography

The only physicist with full academic credentials who devoted himself full-time to investigating the phenomenon from 1966 on. He was the most devastating critic of the 1968 Condon Report, whose methodology he branded institutional manipulation. His death in 1971, recorded as suicide but disputed by relatives and researchers, is listed under pattern 8c, the death of critical witnesses.

Documents

Condon Report · USAF–University of Colorado study

'Scientific Study of Unidentified Flying Objects', a University of Colorado study led by physicist Edward U. Condon under USAF funding from October 1966, delivered in late 1968 and published in January 1969. It examined about 91 cases. Its conclusion, written by Condon, held that UFO study had contributed nothing to science and did not warrant further funding; the National Academy of Sciences endorsed it and the USAF closed Blue Book in December 1969. Two limits: Robert Low's 1966 memo shows a negative result was expected before work began, and about a third of the report's own cases remained unexplained. That residue proves nothing exotic: it may reflect insufficient data.
