138🇦🇺Australia.

Knowles Family · Nullarbor Plain· 1988

On January 20, 1988, around 4:00 am, Faye Knowles and her three adult sons (Patrick 24, Sean 21, Wayne 18) were driving on the Eyre Highway across the Nullarbor Plain when they reported being pursued by a luminous ~1m object described as an egg-in-cup shape. The car was allegedly 'lifted', shaken, set back down, with smoke entering the cabin and altered voices. Ceduna police confirmed the family arrived visibly affected, observed dents on the roof and fine ash, and dismissed any suggestion of a hoax. Subsequent laboratory analyses were mixed: interior dust differed from exterior dust (Dr. Richard Haines, NASA) but additional analyses found no anomalous material. It is the Australian forensic-cohort case closest to the U.S. Cash-Landrum.

1988A45%145🇨🇳China.

China · civil-academic UFO research society (1979)· 1979

On September 20, 1979, students and academics at Wuhan University established the 'China UFO Enthusiasts' Liaison Office' — the first organized Chinese structure dedicated to the study of the UAP phenomenon. In May 1980 it was rebranded as the 'China UFO Research Association' and in February 1981 it began publishing the 'Journal of UFO Research'. The Chinese National Academy of Social Sciences provided moderate institutional support. Its most internationally visible president was Sun Shi Li, a former Chinese diplomat in Mexico and Beijing professor. It is the corpus's oldest Chinese institutional piece and the only non-Western structure in the communist bloc with continuous decades-long academic periodical publication.

1979A70%201🇮🇹Italy.

Zanfretta · Torriglia· 1978–81

On the night of 6 December 1978 night watchman Pier Fortunato Zanfretta radioed his dispatch, in real time, reporting an encounter with an object and beings at Torriglia (Genoa). The Carabinieri certified a physical trace, collected 52 concordant witness statements and filed the case with reliability rated 'good'. The subsequent 11 'abductions' (1978–1981) recovered under hypnosis make it the most documented contact case in Italian ufology — and the most disputed.

1978–81B22%205🇲🇽Mexico.

Carlos de los Santos· 1975

On 3 May 1975, 23-year-old Mexican pilot Carlos Antonio de los Santos flew from Zihuatanejo to Mexico City in his Piper PA-24 and reported three disk-shaped objects that surrounded his aircraft and forced an ascent he felt was beyond his control, until he issued a 'Mayday'. The Mexico City tower logged the incident and radar contact was reported. Astronomer J. Allen Hynek (CUFOS) interviewed the pilot. The later pressure by alleged 'men in black' is the contested layer of the case.

1975B28%208🇵🇱Polonia.

Emilcin · Jan Wolski· 1978

On the morning of 10 May 1978, Polish farmer Jan Wolski, around 70, said that two short, greenish-faced beings climbed onto his horse-drawn cart and led him to a clearing where a white object 'as long as a bus' hovered. He recounted being taken aboard and examined. A man with no profit motive or interest in the subject, his sincerity impressed investigators. In 2005 a monument to the encounter was erected at Emilcin — a rare case of a sighting commemorated in stone.

1978B18%214🇺🇸United States.

The Allagash Abductions· 1976

In August 1976, four art students —twins Jack and Jim Weiner, Charlie Foltz and Chuck Rak— were camping at Eagle Lake, in Maine's Allagash wilderness. They reported a large luminous sphere, a beam of light and a missing-time episode; years later, under fifteen hypnosis sessions, the four separately described an abduction and an examination. The case became an abduction classic — until in 2016 one of them, Chuck Rak, admitted the abduction part was made up.

Mothman · Point Pleasant· 1966–67

Between November 1966 and December 1967, residents of Point Pleasant (West Virginia) reported a large winged creature with red eyes near the former 'TNT Area' explosives site. On 15 December 1967, the Silver Bridge collapsed, killing 46 people; the temporal coincidence fused both events into the 'Mothman' legend.

Maussan · Mummies before the Mexican Congress· 2023

On 12 September 2023, broadcaster Jaime Maussan presented to a hearing of the Mexican Congress two small mummified bodies, with elongated heads and three-fingered hands, which he said were found in Nazca (Peru) and 'not part of our terrestrial evolution'. 2023B10%258🇧🇷Brazil.

Lead Masks · Morro do Vintém· 1966

On 20 August 1966, two Brazilian electronics technicians, Miguel José Viana and Manoel Pereira da Cruz, were found dead on the slope of Morro do Vintém (Niterói), in suits, with raincoats and lead masks over their eyes, with no signs of violence. A note read 'ingest capsules... protect metals, wait for the signal'. The case, tied to spiritualism and 'lights' in the sky, remains unsolved.

1966B18%270🇨🇱Chile.

Friendship Island · Chilean contactee case· 1984

From the mid-1980s, a group of Chilean radio amateurs reported receiving shortwave transmissions from a community identifying itself as 'Friendship'. Its main promoter, Santiago businessman Ernesto de la Fuente, claimed he had been healed of a lung disease by its members. The account described a pacifist community with advanced technology operating from a secret location in southern Chile, associated with a long white vessel photographed in Patagonia. The case, a media phenomenon in Chile in the 1990s (television and press), never produced verifiable evidence of the community or the vessel, and is classified as contactee.

1984B20%300🇺🇸United States.

Majestic 12 · the 'MJ-12' documents and the FBI's BOGUS verdict· 1984–88

The 'Operation Majestic-12' set is the most famous UFO document series and the most thoroughly studied as a forgery. Surfacing between 1984 and 1987 in the hands of ufologists William Moore, Jaime Shandera and Stanton Friedman, the documents describe a secret committee of twelve senior officials (MJ-12) that Truman allegedly created in 1947 to manage crashed UFOs and recovered bodies (Roswell/Aztec). In 1988 two FBI offices received copies of the memo; the Bureau consulted the Air Force (AFOSI), which replied that the documents were 'completely bogus'. The FBI file —available in its reading room, The Vault, under 'Unexplained Phenomenon'— documents that verdict. Document examiners found signatures traced from other letters, anachronistic date formats and inconsistent typography. Its value to the corpus is calibration: it is the document-case that teaches how a 'leaked document' is fabricated and dismantled.

1984–88B8%307🇺🇸United States.

Boyd Bushman · Deathbed Confession· 2014

In October 2014 the posthumous video of Boyd Bushman, a retired Lockheed Martin senior scientist, recorded weeks before his death, was published: he claimed the U.S. employed extraterrestrials at Area 51 and showed photos of an alien as proof. The debunking was immediate and conclusive: the 'alien' was a rubber doll sold at Walmart. The canonical case of unverified aerospace-insider testimony.

2014B8%333🇺🇸United States.

Skinny Bob · viral revival with signs of generative AI (2026)· 2011

●

In May 2011, a grainy video attributed to 1940s-50s U.S. military archives circulated online, showing an extremely thin grey being —nicknamed 'Skinny Bob'— blinking and moving. No one claimed authorship for 13 years. In 2025, self-described former Army intelligence analyst Edward 'Eddie' Abbott stated he had seen the complete, unedited footage inside a SCIF in 2008, with no independent verification. Between May and July 2026, the YouTube channel 'qtecqot' uploaded three new videos —'recovery footage' of the same being and a walkthrough of a craft's interior— structured as an alternate-reality game. An independent technical analysis (a verification bench using ExifTool, C2PA, SynthID and FFmpeg) found specular highlights that stay fixed while underlying geometry moves, a signature generative-AI failure, plus background incoherence — strong evidence of synthetic fabrication disguised as classified 8mm film.

2011B8%336🇺🇸United States.

Cape Girardeau · the family account of a crash six years before Roswell· 1941

In the spring of 1941, Reverend William Guy Huffman —newly arrived pastor at Cape Girardeau's Red Star Baptist Church, Missouri— was called by police to what he was told was a plane crash. According to the account his granddaughter Charlette Mann made public decades later, Huffman found a split-open disc, three small bodies in metallic-looking suits, and unidentified symbols inside; the military cordoned off the site, confiscated a photograph, and demanded silence. For six years it stands as the oldest crashed-craft-with-bodies claim in American ufology —predating Roswell— but with not a single surviving document, photograph, or independent witness to corroborate it.

Two infrared sensor clips, reportedly from April 24, 2024 near Tyndall Air Force Base (Florida), are part of the batch of 50 videos AARO located on a classified network while responding to a FOIA request from eight members of Congress for 51 potentially UAP-related records — a batch AARO itself notes lacks a substantiated chain of custody. The title 'USCG C-144 Tyndall UAP TIC TAC IR hot' that names this case was NOT assigned by the Department of War, but by whoever uploaded the file to the classified network: AARO only assesses it as 'likely' that the sensor belonged to a Coast Guard platform in the Southeastern United States in 2024. AARO's own visual description, more modest than the title, records an 'area of contrast' the sensor manages to track in one clip and loses in the other — not the stable capsule silhouette the title suggests.

The incident the FBI can only show as a digital recreation· 2023

In 2023, a federal law enforcement special agent reported a two-day incident near a sensitive national security site in the western United States. No actual footage of the event exists: what the FBI published, at the Department of War's request in 2026, are two 'digital recreations' —animations based on the agent's first-hand account— catalogued as FBI-UAP-PR005 and PR006, internally titled 'Narrative Statement 3-1' and '3-2.' It is the only case in the corpus where the government itself explicitly acknowledges that the public visual material is not a recording of the event, but a reconstruction made years later from oral testimony.

2018–22B22%381🇺🇸United States.

Chris Bledsoe (Cape Fear River)· 2007

On January 8, 2007, contractor Chris Bledsoe and four witnesses —his 17-year-old son and three subcontractors— reported three orange objects appearing to land near the Cape Fear River in North Carolina, followed that same night by an alleged entity encounter at his home. MUFON investigated with regressive hypnosis, a psychiatric evaluation, and a polygraph that flagged signs of deception on part of Bledsoe's answers; he has since built a public career of books and documentaries that includes the claim —never corroborated by any document or named NASA official— of having been investigated by that agency for eight years.

2007B30%386🇨🇱Chile.

Karim Bianchi · the Chilean senator who says he was abducted· 2012

●

In May 2024, Chilean senator Karim Bianchi (independent, Magallanes) revealed in a television interview that in 2012, while driving from Puerto Natales to Punta Arenas, a spinning light shut off his car and phone, and that he 'appeared' about 200 km further along the same route with no memory of the drive. He described aftereffects —recurring owl sightings, electric shocks when shaking hands for nearly a year— and a visit, days later, from a man who gave him supposed secret NASA documents asking him to 'spread the message'. He stayed silent for over a decade anticipating criticism, and did not retract after the wave of mockery that followed the interview.

2012B18%387🇨🇱Chile.

Pampa Soledad · the artists chased by lights· 1974

In the early hours of 16 August 1974, five Chilean popular artists —among them folk singer Tito Fernández, «El Temucano»— were driving back from a performance at the Oficina Victoria toward Antofagasta when, on the desert stretch of Pampa Soledad, they were followed by an orange light that split into two and then merged into a single object, beneath which they said they saw a tall humanoid figure. They fled at over 130 km/h; a truck driver who crossed their path independently corroborated having been chased by the same lights. The phenomenon stopped once they reached a Carabineros post in Quillagua, where they gave statements. Tito Fernández later wrote «El mensaje de Sirio», describing an alleged subsequent telepathic communication with the entity.
