Aeronautica Militare: Italy's 2014 UFO declassification· 1978–14

Following a wave of sightings in 1978, Prime Minister Giulio Andreotti designated the Aeronautica Militare (Italian Air Force) as the institutional body responsible for receiving and investigating UFO reports in Italy. Decades of files accumulated under 'Secret'/'Reserved' stamps stayed closed until journalists Lao Petrilli and Vincenzo Sinapi gained access to them for the book 'UFO: I dossier italiani' (March 2014), revealing previously unpublished cases —including a radar contact confirmed by a C-130 at Grazzanise (2005)—. Today the Air Force keeps a public year-by-year archive dating to 1972 on its official site, though the institution itself has never framed that routine transparency as a formal act of declassification.

Nuccetelli · the Vandenberg «Red Square»· 2003–05

Between 2003 and 2005, Air Force military police officer Jeffrey Nuccetelli documented five UAP incidents at Vandenberg Air Force Base, California, home of the National Missile Defense Project, during what the National Reconnaissance Office called the 'most important' launches 'in twenty-five years'. The most cited, on October 14, 2003, is known as the 'Vandenberg Red Square': Boeing contractors reported a massive, glowing red square silently hovering over two missile defense sites. That same night, Nuccetelli responded to a second incident involving a rectangular craft seen by five witnesses, and months later he had his own off-duty encounter with a blue-white sphere that appeared and disappeared in the sky. He testified under oath on September 9, 2025, before the House Task Force on the Declassification of Federal Secrets.

Vanguard Enterprise · UAP whistleblower support nonprofit (2026)· 2026

●

In 2026, UAP whistleblowers Dylan Borland (CEO) and Matthew Brown (COO) co-founded Vanguard Enterprise, a nonprofit dedicated to providing legal, medical, financial and psychological support to national-security-sector UAP whistleblowers; whistleblower Jeff Nuccetelli later joined as development director. Under the motto 'Protect the whistleblowers. Disclose the truth,' it is the first organized philanthropic infrastructure specifically for this niche, and institutionally connects three figures already documented in this corpus.
