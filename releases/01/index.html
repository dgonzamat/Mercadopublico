PURSUE · First release

22 cases use documents from this release. The foundational historic drop from the Department of War: FBI and early-USAF files.

On 11 May 2026, three days after the first release, Japan's Chief Cabinet Secretary, Minoru Kihara, addressed it at his regular news conference. The release included two videos recorded near Japan. Kihara said the government is constantly gathering and analyzing information with great interest on incidents related to national security, including UAP, in close coordination with the United States and other countries. Asked whether Japan would disclose similar files, he said decisions would be made case by case, weighing among other factors the risk of exposing its intelligence-gathering capabilities.
