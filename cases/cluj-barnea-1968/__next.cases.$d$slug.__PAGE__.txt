Barnea's own statements to the press in September 1968 do not fully match that account: he said he was with Matea, that there was a group of hikers some distance away in the clearing to whom he did not speak, that he took 'three photographs', and that he was convinced 'the whole time' it was a balloon; only when he developed the film, some two weeks later, did he think it was something else.
