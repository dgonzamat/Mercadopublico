In the Gheorghiță and Hobana version, the object advanced slowly, changed direction and brightness, then abruptly accelerated, climbed and vanished; Barnea took four photos, the last with the object already reduced to a dot, and it was 13:23. Barnea's own statements to the press in September 1968 do not fully match that account: he said he was with Matea, that there was a group of hikers some distance away in the clearing to whom he did not speak, that he took 'three photographs', and that he was convinced 'the whole time' it was a balloon; only when he developed the film, some two weeks later, did he think it was something else. In that same interview, however, he said that the object, metallic in sheen and silent, 'did not resemble any of the bodies I have seen so many times, from balloons to turbojets', and that it 'vanished suddenly, vertically, in less than a minute', an ending that did not fit a balloon. The time given in those 1968 reports does not match either, ranging from 13:15 to 15:50.
