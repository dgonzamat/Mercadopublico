32:T450,That said, analytical honesty splits the posterior between indeterminacy and the mundane/misid narrative, without loading onto the non-human. There is a fact in the record itself that pulls strongly toward the prosaic: the acknowledged presence of jets and helicopters over the reservoir that same night. Aircraft lights maneuvering at night over frozen water —with reflections on the ice, loss of distance cues and the autokinetic effect on a bright point light— reproduce much of what was described (the 'angular' movements, 'straight' ascents, 'rocking-chair' oscillation). A bright astronomical object low on the horizon could have seeded the first reports, then amplified by social contagion once the story reached the press. What sustains the indeterminacy mass is that none of those pieces cleanly explains the totality —the duration, the cylindrical shape over the dam, the October recurrence— and that the case never received a conclusive official investigation.
