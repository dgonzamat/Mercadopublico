14:T484,On October 30, 2024, according to the congressman who presented the video, two U.S. MQ-9 Reapers operating off the coast of Yemen — an active theater for anti-Houthi operations against drone and missile attacks on commercial shipping — tracked an unidentified orb which, per the footage presented, was moving in a straight line low over the sea surface. Burlison explained that the Hellfire was fired by a second MQ-9 not seen in the video; the image carries the label "LRD LASE DES", which according to The War Zone indicates that the filming MQ-9 was laser-designating the target for the other. The video, about 50 seconds long, shows the projectile entering from the upper left and striking the orb, which sheds some debris; the missile deflects away without detonating and the object continues on its path until the clip ends. "It kept going, and it looked like the debris was taken with it," Burlison said. "I'm not going to speculate what it is."
