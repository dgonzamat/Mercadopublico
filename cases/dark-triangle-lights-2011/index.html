🇺🇸2011·U.S. residential area (undisclosed location)

United States.U.S. · two people in a car see a huge black triangle

One night in June or July 2011, two of the three people in a car leaving a driveway watched a massive black triangle pass low overhead heading north, a car-sized white light at each corner, emitting a low, pulsing hum. One of them estimated its dimensions at roughly 200 by 100 feet and thought it might have been 800 feet up. The object disappeared behind the treeline in 10-15 seconds, leaving no contrail, before they could film it. The FBI took both witnesses' statements in April 2025, nearly fourteen years after the event.

Why no ordinary cause is identified
No source proposes a cause: the FBI FD-302 (FBI-UAP-D028) records the two witnesses' testimony without assessment, and the PURSUE release publishes it as unresolved. The absence of a published analysis is not evidence against an ordinary cause: in its 2024 report AARO stated that "all 174 cases have been finalized as resolved to prosaic objects including balloons, birds, UAS, satellites, and aircraft." The same report adds that "many other cases remain unresolved," so the figure is the rate among resolved cases, not among all cases. (source ↗)

What happened

One night in June or July 2011, after dark, the first of the two witnesses was pulling out of his driveway around 8 or 9 PM. He was not driving: he was with two other people, the driver and a companion in the front passenger seat. The form does not say where he was sitting. The vehicle was about fifty feet from the driveway, preparing to turn left at the intersection. As they reached the corner, the first witness was looking out the window and saw three lights in the sky. He got out of the vehicle, and looking up, saw a massive black triangular object flying north. The object had a car-sized white light at each of its three corners, slightly recessed inward, emitting a diffuse glow. It flew low, producing a low humming noise with a pulsing sound.

One of the occupants told him to get a video, but by the time he got his phone out, the object had already flown over the treeline behind the houses to the north and out of sight. They watched it for a total of about 10 to 15 seconds. According to the FBI report, it left no contrails, and the first witness felt dumbfounded and awestruck.

The estimates come from the second witness, whom the form quotes separately. He was overwhelmed by the object's size and by how low it was. He thought it might have been 800 feet above the ground and, from the dimensions of their surroundings, estimated it at roughly 200 feet long by 100 feet wide. At first glance it looked to him like an equilateral triangle, but the dimensions he calculated fit an isosceles triangle better. The object moved slowly and steadily, and he too heard the pulsing hum. Besides the three corner lights, he recalled something grayish or graphite-colored in the middle of the triangle. The form does not say whether this second witness was the driver or the companion.

Neither witness had a clear memory of the object's thickness or other details of its vertical profile: they explained that at first they were underneath the triangle and did not see its side well, and that when they could have seen its rear better they were trying, without success, to get a phone out to record it.

The second witness believed a couple of people in the neighborhood had also seen it. Both said they would try to find out whether someone whose name is blacked out, as well as the neighbors who saw it, would be willing to give a written statement or speak with the FBI.

The two witnesses were interviewed together by FBI agents on April 11, 2025, nearly fourteen years after the sighting, and the interview was documented in an FD-302, the FBI's form for such interviews. It was conducted by two special agents and a task force officer, with a fourth person present whose title is blacked out. According to the form, one witness served six and a half years in the U.S. Army and the other 24 years; the rest of what it says about their careers is blacked out.

Why it matters

The FD-302 was written so long after the sighting that everything it says is memory: the witnesses recorded nothing, there is no photograph, and the form is printed with a note that it contains neither recommendations nor conclusions of the FBI. The two witnesses were interviewed together. The document attributes much of what they say to one or the other, with the names blacked out, but in a joint interview it is not possible to know how much of each account is independent of the other. The vehicle's third occupant does not appear as a declarant.

The measurements are the second witness's estimates: the altitude, about 800 feet, which he thought possible, and the size, about 200 by 100 feet, which he calculated from the dimensions of the surroundings; neither was measured. The FD-302 records that it left no contrail, which says little, because contrails form at high altitude and would barely be visible at night. The second witness believed a couple of neighbors had also seen it; the file does not confirm any.
