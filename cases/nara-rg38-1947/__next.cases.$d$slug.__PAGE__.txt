10:T4a5,The Muroc episode is documented, but through another channel. On July 8, 1947, the same day as the Roswell press release, several military personnel and civilians at Muroc Army Air Field (today Edwards AFB), which was an Army base and not a naval air station, saw silver objects. Lieutenant Joseph McHenry and three subordinates described at around 10:00 two spherical or disc-like objects at about 8,000 feet and 300 to 400 mph, and then a third flying in tight circles; at noon Major Richard Shoop saw a thin metallic object oscillating and climbing for about eight minutes; at 11:50 Captain John Paul Stapp, waiting for a P-82 ejection-seat test, saw a white oval object fall like a parachute and judged it man-made, and the base commander, Colonel Gilkey, stated that what he saw looked like paper of no significance. Those sworn statements were taken by the Muroc CIC agent and sent by the base intelligence officer to the A-2 section of Fourth Air Force at Hamilton Field, whose report of August 18, 1947, opened at the request of Air Defense Command, concludes that no further investigation is being considered.
