12:T463,Kovalyonok had just finished an exercise session when, through an observation porthole, he saw in front of the spacecraft an object flying on a parallel trajectory. The station was overflying southern Africa heading toward the Indian Ocean. In his later accounts, Kovalyonok stressed that the object was small in angular appearance —comparable to the size of a finger held up before the eye—, initially elliptical. By his description, as he watched, the body became translucent, showed an internal structure, and then separated into two connected pieces, 'a bit like dumbbells' (dumbbell/barbell). At one end a gas- or plasma-like emission appeared — Kovalyonok described it as 'reactive object'-like. In the account he gave Michael Hesemann (2002) he tells it differently: the elliptical object flew alongside them, there was an explosion 'of golden light', a second explosion one or two seconds later and two golden spheres appeared; then he saw only white smoke, and when the station entered the Earth's shadow he could no longer see them.
