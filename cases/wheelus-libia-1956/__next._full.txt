3a:T71e,The card (ATIC Form 329) gives October 18, 1956, date-time group 17/2217Z, military source, two objects, course "varied" and ground-radar and air-intercept observation; it carries the summary speaking of a "solid object" rather than weather or anomalous propagation, the "Unknown" box smudged, "WEATHER RETURNS" typed in a different typeface above the "Other" line, which is crossed by an ink stroke, and the comments section covered by a black bar. The next five cards are an extract, of February 6, 1957, of report IR-1-56 from the 7272d Air Base Wing Combat Operations Center (October 29, 1956; evaluation B-2; sources, the 633d AC&W and the 431st Fighter Interceptor Squadron), secret and later declassified by Hector Quintanilla; it leaves out several items of the five-page original (it jumps, for instance, from 9.b(1) to 9.b(3)). It gives the ground-radar positions as grid references (the first object at NJPD 5047 and, at the turn, NJPD 2805; the second at NJQD 0739) and, for each F-86D interception, the time, heading, bearing and range. On the second object: F/Blue 1 picks it up on a heading of 050° and gets "Splash" on a bearing of 049°; F/Blue 2 picks it up on a heading of 100° at bearing 042°, with "Judy" at 047° and "Splash" at 050°.
