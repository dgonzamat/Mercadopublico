33:T440,Its second importance is as a case study of the institutional failure that defines the Blue Book era. Quintanilla's 'investigation' —two minutes on the phone, an attempt to have a false duration signed, an Echo-then-Venus explanation physically incompatible with an hour-long chase— is the textbook example of how the official program closed cases to manage public perception rather than to understand the phenomenon. That it took congressional pressure to force an in-person interview, and that the program's own scientific advisor (Hynek) documented the handling as evidence of method, makes Portage County a key piece in the file on distrust: it does not prove the object was anomalous, but it does prove the apparatus tasked with explaining it was not trying to. The cost fell on the witness: Spaur, an officer with no agenda, lost credibility, family and career for reporting what he saw.
