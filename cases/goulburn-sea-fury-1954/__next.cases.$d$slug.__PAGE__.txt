18:T40a,The Navy's file, now in the National Archives of Australia, shows how the report was handled. Nowra sent it to the Director of Naval Intelligence in Melbourne, who passed it to Air Force Intelligence, the body that collated such reports. The Navy asked the RAAF whether it had aircraft near Goulburn; the answer was no, but a TAA airline Convair was in the area at 14,000 feet on a northerly course. Naval Intelligence's assessment, of 1 October, concluded that O'Farrell was an entirely credible witness, that he saw 'a mass associated with the lights' but was unable to distinguish any form, that the night was clear, starlit and moonless, that the pilot, visibly shaken, remained adamant that he had seen the objects, and that there was no question of collusion between the two witnesses. The committee set up to study such reports thanked him for 'such a well authenticated sighting'.
