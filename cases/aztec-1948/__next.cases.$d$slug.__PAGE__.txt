The contrast with Roswell is deliberately instructive: it shows that two accounts of almost identical structure can have opposite epistemic status depending on the evidence, and that the 'crash-retrieval' label does not by itself confer credibility. In the corpus, Aztec is the case that teaches one to ask for the metal sample before believing in the craft."}]
