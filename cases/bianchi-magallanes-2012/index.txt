2a:T642,In May 2024, the talk show 'Not News' on the Chilean channel Vía X, hosted by Nicolás Larraín, aired an interview with Senator Karim Bianchi Retamales, independent representative for the 15th Circumscription (Magallanes Region) since 2022, in which he publicly recounted for the first time an episode he said he experienced in 2012, when he was a regional councillor and worked at Radio Soberanía. By his account, he was driving between Puerto Natales and Punta Arenas, alone, at night and talking on the phone, when 'a spinning light approached, large, a saucer'; to Chilevisión he described it as 'a very large light, circular in shape, of different colors.' The vehicle shut off entirely —engine, lights, radio and the phone he was talking on— and the next thing he recalls is 'appearing' about 200 kilometers further on, with no memory of the stretch in between; in a later Instagram video he added: 'it was hard to start the car and I got back on the road.' He resumed the call with the same person to tell them 'I've arrived.' He placed the encounter 'at the height of the Rubens', on Route 9, about 60 kilometers from Natales and about 185 from Punta Arenas, but the sources contradict each other on the direction of travel: to BioBioChile he said 'I was on my way to Puerto Natales from Punta Arenas', which would leave him about 60 kilometers from his destination, while Infobae's version implies the opposite direction, so the 200 kilometers cannot be checked against the route. Bianchi has not publicly identified the person he was speaking with nor produced any record of the call.2b:T470,By his account, the episode had aftereffects. When shaking hands, he said, he 'gave something like electric shocks for many months, almost a year I'd say.' He also began noticing owls everywhere —an owl given to him as a gift, a friend's owl T-shirt, a restaurant called 'Las lechuzas', a bedspread with owl eyes—, something he narrowed in another interview to 'for a month'; an 'expert' told him it was linked to extraterrestrials 'because of the turn of the neck and the eyes.'
