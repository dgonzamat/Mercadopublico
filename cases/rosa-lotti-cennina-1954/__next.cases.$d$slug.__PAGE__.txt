16:T744,She described it as a large spindle fixed vertically in the ground, a kind of double cone —'like two bells joined at their bases'— whose surface shone like very polished metal. In the lower cone was an open glass door, through which two small seats could be seen; in the central, widest part there was a kind of rounded glass following the machine's shape. As she watched, from behind the object emerged two beings a little over a metre tall, of friendly appearance —the elder one laughing— dressed in suits resembling a pilot's. They spoke an incomprehensible language, with sounds that seemed Chinese to her: 'liu, lai, loi'. The two beings snatched the carnations and one of her black stockings; when Rosa protested timidly, the elder returned some flowers but kept five, examined them curiously while laughing, wrapped them in the stocking and threw them inside the spindle through the small opening. Frightened, Rosa fled; when she returned with neighbours, the object and the beings had vanished. The press of the time carries a sparer version: according to L'Unità of 3 November, in her complaint to the carabinieri she wrote that at Poggio d'Ambra, in the municipality of Bucine, two strangers a metre tall, in a grey suit «shaped like a diving suit», a leather helmet, a normal face and very small teeth, took five red carnations and «a black cup» from her, and that some five metres away stood a metal double-cone device about two metres tall, with a glass door and two small seats. The paper added that in the village she was regarded as «perfectly sane, calm and serious», that she ran to the village shouting, and that the carabinieri and villagers who went to the spot found neither the device nor its occupants, and that «a careful examination of the ground did not yield even a small trace» of the device or of their footsteps.17:T42e,The case fits the dominant pattern of the 1954 wave, in which most reported entities were human-like 'dwarfs', often in diver-type suits or, as here, a pilot's.
