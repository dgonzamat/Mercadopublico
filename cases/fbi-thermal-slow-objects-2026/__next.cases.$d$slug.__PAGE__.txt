14:T5d2,In 2026, an employee at a sensitive federal site, whose position is redacted in the statement —with no aviation expertise, though with some private pilot time—, was accompanied by, among others, a Special Agent when, at approximately 1604 hours, he was alerted to a radio-frequency detection: a 5-to-10-second burst emission at roughly 10,000 feet MSL, on an initially unknown bearing. Further communication clarified a request to observe on bearing 58 degrees. Within a few minutes, the witness aimed a thermal optical device in that direction and saw a black object in the sky, moving slowly. He took photographs using the optical device itself. While observing, a second object appeared to the right of the first, and he took additional photos and video. Both objects moved together, at the same speed, and both were 'black hot' in the thermal image: in that polarity the hottest things render black, so the objects were warmer than the background. He lost sight of them as they moved out of range. Total observation time was about 5 to 10 minutes; he could not estimate exact altitude or speed. The objects were flying above the mountain line, and the statement gives their direction as southeast, 'approximately 58 degrees to 62 degrees', while adding that, given the distance, it was difficult for him to determine it. He was the only person present who saw them, and the only one who used the optical device; the photographs were provided to the FBI agent who took his statement.15:T621,The following day, at about 2100 hours, an FBI Special Agent, positioned in the same general observation area 'referenced in my prior statement', according to his signed statement, observed and filmed a thermally 'elevated' aerial object —that is, hot, like the objects from the day before; the agent calls that sighting 'the previous night', although the alert had come in at about 1604— in the same general western sector and on a similar heading. The object appeared roughly two ridgelines farther west than the prior day's observation point; given the greater distance and terrain separation, the agent could not identify specific physical characteristics or determine its exact configuration, but the object presented as hot on thermal observation and appeared to be flying southbound, consistent with the general direction observed the previous day. During the same operational period, the agent also recorded video of a U.S. Army Black Hawk helicopter operating in the same general area, with the explicit purpose of establishing a visual and thermal reference point for scale, flight characteristics, and comparative size assessment. Based on that comparison, the unidentified object appeared to be approximately half the size of the Black Hawk —an estimate based solely on observed appearance, distance, and available reference footage.
