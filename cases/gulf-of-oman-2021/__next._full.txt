2e:T428,Over the rest of the sortie, the crew logged approximately 25 instances in total of these objects, seen in infrared as cold orbs in both white-hot and black-hot mode, which are two ways of displaying the same thermal image. Two of them flew in a loose but coordinated formation, rising and falling 'like a wave' while maneuvering around, over and under each other 'in a manner similar to how dolphins playfully swim in a pod'; the report adds a source comment: 'They knew they were flying together.' Others were seen in a formation of three, and others flying erratically and maneuvering aggressively.
