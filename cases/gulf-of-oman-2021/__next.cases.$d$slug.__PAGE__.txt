18:T428,Over the rest of the sortie, the crew logged approximately 25 instances in total of these objects, seen in infrared as cold orbs in both white-hot and black-hot mode, which are two ways of displaying the same thermal image. Two of them flew in a loose but coordinated formation, rising and falling 'like a wave' while maneuvering around, over and under each other 'in a manner similar to how dolphins playfully swim in a pod'; the report adds a source comment: 'They knew they were flying together.' Others were seen in a formation of three, and others flying erratically and maneuvering aggressively. The crew tried to save all the footage, but the aircraft's digital video recorder 'inexplicably failed' and the data became corrupted; even so, the report attached videos that, in its own words, depict a smoothly slewing or a stationary sensor with 'multiple UAPs' darting around the feed. Using the onboard tactical tool (TACTOOL), the CSO calculated a maximum maneuvering speed of 1,300 miles per hour and a minimum of 250.
