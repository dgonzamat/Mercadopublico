4b:T4c5,On Sunday 10 October 1993, at about 00:15, two people were driving along the road from Zafra to Llerena, in the province of Badajoz. About three kilometres from the Usagre junction, according to the statement one of them wrote a few days later, they saw with absolute clarity, for several seconds, something he described, for want of a better term, as a kind of spacecraft. It appeared lit up about a hundred metres to the left of the road, in open country. It was oval; they could not see the lower part because it was hidden by trees or a mound, but they did see three quarters of its body, outlined by numerous orange, yellow and white lights. They lingered in the area for about half an hour, torn between curiosity and fear, and roughly every five minutes they saw a flash that lit up the outline of the horizon in all directions. They thought of a distant storm, but it did not seem easy to equate with lightning. At 00:45 they drove on to Llerena.
