5f:T41c,Kecksburg forces apart three layers the case's folklore tends to fuse. The first is astronomical and solid: there was a real bolide, documented by hundreds of witnesses, pilots, photographs and a seismograph, whose reconstructed trajectory ended over Lake Erie, more than 300 km from the Kecksburg woods. The impression that a bolide falls "just behind the hill" a few kilometers away is a classic estimation error, and the 1966 analysis itself warns about it. The second is institutional and also solid, but says less than is usually attributed to it: the Blue Book file proves the Air Force searched for a fallen object together with the state police, and recorded as its result that nothing was found. The military deployment that popular memory presents as an anomaly therefore has a documented, contemporaneous explanation: they searched for what residents had reported.
