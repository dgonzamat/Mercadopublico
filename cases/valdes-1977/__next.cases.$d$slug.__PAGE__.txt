15:T452,The case has two layers, and they are worth keeping apart. The first is what the whole group saw. Rosales, on guard with Reyes, saw a light come down behind some hills; Valdés first thought of a truck coming down the road or a meteorite, and as it fell, 'the light made an incandescent flash behind the hill'. Shortly afterwards a second object appeared about 500 metres away, ovoid and violet, with two red lights on its sides that Valdés compares to beacons. The horses, more than three hundred, bolted, and the group, unarmed according to NCO Antonio Flores, huddled together to pray. No source proposes an explanation for that second object: Valdés maintains that the phenomenon existed, says he still has no answer for those lights and adds that they 'have more of the human than of the outside about them'. This layer does not arrive clean either: the only record of that night is the collective account that teacher Pedro Araneda recorded hours later, and according to Alejandro Agostinelli, Araneda showed the soldiers pictures of flying saucers 'to help them recognise the shape of the object'.16:T4bd,The second layer is the one that made the case famous. Valdés stepped away from the group and his companions lost sight of him for about fifteen minutes; when he reappeared he collapsed, seemed to be in a trance and said: 'You will never know who we are or where we come from, but we will return soon'. At six in the morning he went to fetch NCO Antonio Flores in Putre and told him: 'It seems I fell asleep, and when the conscripts woke me it was chaos'; according to Flores, he did not mention having disappeared: 'The soldiers said that'. That same afternoon Araneda, a UFO enthusiast, recorded the only group interview with the soldiers, and in that collective account the beard of several days and the watch calendar showing 30 April emerged; it was Araneda who linked the beard and the watch to a 'time distortion'. According to the newspaper El Día, the Army tasked a Corporal Roca that same 25 April with interviewing all the conscripts to look for contradictions, and he found none; that file, described as 'secret', has not been published.
