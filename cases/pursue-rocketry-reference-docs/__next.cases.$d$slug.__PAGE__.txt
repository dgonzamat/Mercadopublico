15:T58d,That said, their value to the corpus is real and twofold. First, they make the resolution apparatus visible: the less glamorous but more substantive side of any serious UAP-study program is the library of known mundane causes —rocketry, balloons, air traffic, atmospheric phenomena— against which reports are checked. That the state releases these materials alongside the sightings confirms that the 'unidentified' category is, in large part, a problem of pending identification, not of exotic technology. Second, they empirically anchor the corpus's mundane narrative: many coastal and range cases that resolve into launches or reentries find their documentary substrate here. The file enters as a tier-B document-case with a deliberately low anomaly probability, not because its content is weak, but because its content is, quite intentionally, prosaic: it is the rule against which the exception is measured. Distinguishing the report of the phenomenon from the technical reference that explains it is, at bottom, the minimal discipline separating a reasoned catalog of anomalies from a pile of PDFs unified only by a common administrative label.
