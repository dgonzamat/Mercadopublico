58:T485,The replies came within six days. On 11 March the embassy in Rabat reported that the newspaper Le Matin had said hundreds of Casablanca residents saw two greenish lights west of the city and, the next day, that the same phenomenon was seen over Rabat, Fez and northern Spain, which the paper put down to a conjunction of Venus with Sirius.
