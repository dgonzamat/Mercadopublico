5:T43b,In March 1952, Captain Edward J. Ruppelt — director of the Air Technical Intelligence Center (ATIC) Project Blue Book at Wright-Patterson AFB — gave a 'flying saucer' lecture to scientists at MIT Lincoln Laboratory (a FFRDC founded in 1951 at Hanscom AFB, Massachusetts). The talk was captured on reel-to-reel tape and catalogued as 'AF-ATIC-FILM, 03/52.' The recording remained in MIT Lincoln Lab's possession for 74 years. On June 24, 2026, Rep. Eric Burlison (R-MO) confirmed that MIT Lincoln Lab acknowledged the file's existence and agreed to hand it over to Congress. It is the first known recording of an official USAF briefing on UAP/flying saucers to civilian defense contractors, made four months before the 'Invasion of Washington' of July 1952.
