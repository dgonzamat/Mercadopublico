17:T53e,In March 1952, Captain Edward J. Ruppelt — head of Project Blue Book at the Air Technical Intelligence Center (ATIC) of the USAF, based at Wright-Patterson AFB, Ohio — visited MIT Lincoln Laboratory at Hanscom AFB (Lexington, Massachusetts) to deliver a lecture on 'flying saucer' phenomena. MIT Lincoln Lab had been founded in 1951 as a federally funded research and development center (FFRDC) with an air defense mission, and its scientists were key interlocutors for aerial threat analysis. The lecture was captured on reel-to-reel tape and catalogued as 'AF-ATIC-FILM, 03/52' — the medium is worth pinning down at the outset, because the catalogue code says FILM while the letter demanding it describes a sound recording. Ruppelt, who would coin the term 'UFO' in 1952 and serve as Blue Book director until 1953, represented at that time the USAF's most systematic effort to analyze and catalog sightings of unidentified aerial phenomena. The context is significant: in March 1952, the ATIC was processing a wave of military and civilian sightings that would peak four months later with the 'Invasion of Washington' of July 1952 — sightings over D.C. airspace that sent F-94 interceptors after radar-visible objects over the White House and Capitol, generating the largest UAP media coverage in the United States up to that point.18:T4f4,The tape stayed archived at MIT Lincoln Lab for 74 years without being published. On June 9, 2026, the bipartisan Capitol press conference revealed that Rep. Eric Burlison had sent the laboratory a formal letter, dated May 7, 2026, whose opening line is the most precise description of the object in existence: 'I write concerning a reel-to-reel recording identified as “AF-ATIC-FILM, 03/52” from the Beacon Hill Study, labeled “flying saucer talk,” with Edward J. Ruppelt listed as the briefer.' The letter asks that it be located, preserved, digitized to preservation grade and transferred to the National Archives for inclusion in Record Group 615, and expressly extends the request to 'audio recordings, motion-picture film, transcripts, briefing materials' and other material relating to the Beacon Hill Study. On June 24, 2026 it was reported that MIT Lincoln Lab had confirmed the file's existence and agreed to hand it over, and Burlison stated so publicly at the June 25 Disclosure Forum 2026 (Kennedy Caucus Room, Russell Senate Office Building). According to Burlison, the lab's attorneys responded quickly and said they would comply within 30 days. Burlison indicated the material is 'likely' connected to the Washington D.C.
