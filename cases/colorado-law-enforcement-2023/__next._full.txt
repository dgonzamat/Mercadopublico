28:T4df,Alongside the four videos the release carries a transcript of what the officer was saying as he filmed: document LLE-UAP-D001. It is one page, and in it he identifies himself, gives the time, the mile marker and his direction of travel — all redacted — and describes what he sees: 'I am watching another orb that is flying approximately... 300 feet up off the ground. I did check radar, there is no aircraft flying in the area.' Fifteen seconds later he adds: 'this has been flying stationary for about 15 minutes.' A minute in, as a large truck goes past: 'Okay, the craft is flying closer to me now. I can see it.' There the published transcript ends. The redactions carry the (b)(7)(A) and (b)(7)(C) exemptions of the Freedom of Information Act, the ones protecting ongoing law-enforcement proceedings and personal privacy; the preliminary note says it was edited 'to protect the identity of the source, as well as law enforcement activities.'
