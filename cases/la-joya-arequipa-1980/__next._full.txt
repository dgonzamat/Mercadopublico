29:T64a,No instrumental record survives: no gun-camera film or published radar tracks are preserved, and the Peruvian Air Force published no report, which fed both its reputation as a 'perfect' case and the doubts of skeptics. There is, however, a contemporaneous record, from another state: intelligence report IR 6 876 0146 80, 'UFO sighted in Peru', prepared by Col. Norman H. Runge at the U.S. defense attaché office in Lima with a date of information of May 10, 1980, and received at the Joint Chiefs of Staff on June 3. Its source is an FAP officer 'who observed the event'. According to the report, the object was spotted on two occasions near Mariano Melgar Air Base at La Joya: first on the morning of May 9, when a group of officers in formation saw a UFO 'round in shape, hovering near the airfield'; the air commander scrambled an Su-22 and the pilot, 'according to a third party', intercepted it and fired upon it 'at very close range without causing any apparent damage'; on a second pass the UFO out-ran the Su-22. The second, in the early evening of May 10: the vehicle was lighted, another Su-22 was scrambled, and again it out-ran the aircraft.
