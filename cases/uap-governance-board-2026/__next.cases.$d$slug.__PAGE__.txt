1a:T59b,The UAP Governance Board is the highest-ranking institutional control node in the Trump administration's 2026 UAP disclosure architecture, positioned between the presidential directive (Feb 2026), the PURSUE portal (May–Jun 2026), AARO, and the SAC. Its analytical weight is threefold. First, the GB centralizes declassification authority: while PURSUE published three tranches of reviewed files (May–Jun 2026) and the SAC deliberates on unclassified data, the GB controls what moves from the classified to the public regime, at what pace, and in what format — it is the information gate. Second, the anonymity of its members is a design signal, not an omission: ODNI declared the charter but named no one, reproducing the opacity model that characterizes IC components (SIGINT, HUMINT) vis-à-vis public scrutiny. A GB operating without public nomination means it is not politically accountable to media or the disclosure ecosystem, only to the interagency chain of command. Third, the classified/unclassified asymmetry between the GB and the SAC is analytically revealing: it places the SAC in a position to legitimize the disclosure process without having access to the substance being disclosed. If the GB retains significant material, the SAC cannot detect it; if the GB releases curated material, the SAC receives it as if complete.
