13:T5b8,The record is a CIA information report ("Information Report, Not Finally Evaluated Intelligence") with a distribution date of July 3, 2008, classified SECRET//NOFORN, whose subject is the placement of a redacted entity "on high alert due to perceived aggressive foreign posturing," with a date of information of "early July 2008." The document does not say that the report reached the White House Situation Room or that it put CIA assets in Zimbabwe on alert. The Situation Room appears as one of roughly fifty addressees on a standard distribution list (DNI, State Department, DIA, NSA, FBI, FAA, regional commands), with no sign of special handling. And what was placed on high alert was not U.S. assets: the text says that, regardless of its origins, the incident "had resulted in the decision to place the Zimbabwe" entity, whose name is redacted, "on high alert." Nor is the debate one among CIA analysts: it is "individuals," their identification redacted, "who were aware of the incident" who debated whether the object was "an advanced reconnaissance device belonging to a foreign government, or whether the object was an unidentified flying object of extraterrestrial origins." The most natural reading is that the report's source is describing the reaction of Zimbabwe's security apparatus, which in the middle of an electoral crisis read the object as foreign surveillance.
