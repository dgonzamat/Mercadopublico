16:T5f3,The video known as 'Skinny Bob' appeared online in April–May 2011: four black-and-white videos uploaded on April 14 and May 2, 9 and 18 by the anonymous account 'ivan0135', created on April 13 (the third carries no footage, only text messages), presented as leaked film from a series of interviews, conducted between 1942 and 1969, with a grey being from Zeta Reticuli. They showed an extremely thin being, bald, with a disproportionate head, narrow mouth and large blinking eyes, seated at a table, full-length, and lying in a kind of pool. The often-repeated KGB attribution is not a later accretion: the uploader added it himself, since the first 2011 video opens with a still of a 'TOP SECRET KGB ARCHIVE' logo lifted from the 1998 documentary 'Secret KGB UFO Files'. For years the community analyzed it frame by frame. On the Metabunk forum and on skinnybob.info, the scratch-and-grain layer was identified as a film-damage overlay sold on commercial stock sites (Pond5) and available online by about 2009; the on-screen timecode typeface turned out to be Consolas, released by Microsoft in 2006-2007, and the aging is consistent with Boris FX's Sapphire plug-ins for After Effects. In addition, the aging effects also degrade the timecode, so they were applied last, the opposite of what happens with film shot and then transferred. The uploader attributes the material to a range from 1942 to 1957; the frames carry a burned-in timecode in the lower-left corner with a black block right over the date digits.
