An independent analysis assembled a full verification bench on the new qtecqot videos: yt-dlp to preserve native resolution and framerate, ExifTool for container metadata, a C2PA content-provenance manifest check, Google's SynthID detector, FFmpeg to examine grain and specular highlights frame by frame, and reverse image search for commercial film-damage overlays. Most of those tests were inconclusive by the material's own design —YouTube strips most container metadata on re-upload, the absence of a C2PA manifest proves nothing, and SynthID only detects Google's own generators, missing Kling, Runway, Veo or local models. But the pixel-level analysis did produce a strong signal: specular highlights that stay fixed while the underlying geometry shifts, a generation failure characteristic of current AI video models, along with background incoherence —repeated cones, foliage that morphs between frames, an implausible body gait.
