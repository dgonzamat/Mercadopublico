A woman saw it from another street with her five-year-old son. McCahey took the five statements the next day, judged them reliable and attached clippings from The Advertiser reporting sightings from other parts of South Australia that same night. The security officer proposed no cause: he wrote that it would appear 'an object of cigar shape, whether man made or not, was observed'. Several witnesses ruled out a shooting star on their own account: one said it was 'definitely something other than a shooting star', another that it travelled horizontally, faster than any aircraft but not as fast as a falling star, and the baker Joseph Ager had 'no hesitation' in saying it was man-made.
