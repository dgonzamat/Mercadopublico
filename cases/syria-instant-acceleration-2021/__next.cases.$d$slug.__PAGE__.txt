On February 3, 2026, three and a half months before the official release, Jeremy Corbell published it on YouTube under the title "SYRIA UAP 2021 : Military-Filmed Footage / Apparent Instantaneous Acceleration", in a version without the official redactions. The description that accompanied it, quoted in the Metabunk thread opened that same day, attributes the footage to an MQ-9 Reaper with an MTS-B sensor operating under the direction of the U.S. Air Force, places the shot in Syria from the Jordanian border, giving coordinates (32°05' N, 36°53' E) on the Jordanian side, and says the object was tracked with a "weapons-quality lock", showed abrupt directional changes, instantaneous acceleration and "intelligent control", and that the absence of traditional propulsion or thermal signatures was noted in documentation. The leaked version appears to have been filmed off a screen; the official one, according to Mick West, is the original footage re-encoded, and it includes nearly a minute (from 3:11 to 4:05) missing from the leak, in which the object appears to turn around.
