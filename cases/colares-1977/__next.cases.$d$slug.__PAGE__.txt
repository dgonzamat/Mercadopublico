3c:T647,Its executive summary on Colares (March 30, 2009) is not a record of the period: it condenses eight binders of notes by a private investigator and a U.S. scientist on more than 370 on-site investigations and 514 "victims" across the country between 1977 and 2003, and it starts from a premise the 1977 record does not contain: "a terror that comes from some place other than Earth." Its figures go far beyond the military record: ten deaths after prolonged illness, 54 people burned, and a doctor, her name redacted and "located in Belem," who reportedly treated about 40 injured people from Colares in 1977 and found "low levels of hemoglobin" in all the burn patients she tested. In 1977 the Colares doctor told the military team she had observed no anemia; the summary does not make clear whether they are the same person.
