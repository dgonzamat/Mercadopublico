The hydrophobicity and ring shape have a serious prosaic candidate —a reproducible fungal fairy ring— which lowers the anomalous load; but the persistent glow and the object sighting do not fit cleanly into that explanation. Faruk's chemical re-analysis adds an interesting twist: the organic compound that makes the soil hydrophobic would, per his work, also be responsible for the luminescence, which could reconcile the glow with a chemical-biological rather than exotic origin. That very possibility shows why the case does not rise to Tier S: the closer chemistry comes to explaining the trace, the weaker the need for an anomalous cause, and the object sighting rests on a single teenager.
