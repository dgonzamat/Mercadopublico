4c:T468,On December 31, 2007, in the early morning, residents of Mezcala (a town in the Eduardo Neri municipality, in Guerrero, Mexico) saw strange lights in the sky. According to the testimonies gathered by ufologist Ana Luisa Cid, three luminous objects appeared: two medium-sized ones which, according to Alfonso Ramírez Maya, the resident who filmed them, were spinning, and a larger one, an ovoid with bluish tones, which stayed over the Pie de Minas hill, motionless and giving off strong flashes, until January 2, 2008: nearly 72 hours, according to Cid. The press report closest to the event, Francisco Rangel Cruz's piece in La Crónica of Chilpancingo on January 10, 2008, gives a shorter duration: the light flashed over the hill for "more than 30 hours", and the population "could not sleep for two nights in a row". According to that report, the light first passed over the main square at about 300 meters, and from the hill it could be seen "even by day emitting strong flashes upward".
