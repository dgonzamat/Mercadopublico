24:T4bf,The exception to the case's purely narrative nature is his death. 25:T4da,Dulce/Schneider matters as an archetype, not as evidence: it is the textbook case of the underground-base (DUMB) 'insider witness' — a convincing speaker, a specific story of combat with non-humans, scars displayed as proof — with not a single verifiable piece behind it. Its value to the corpus is twofold. First, it anchors in a single file the family of 'insider' claims that runs through UAP culture, making its pattern explicit: unverified credentials, absence of a documentary trail for events that would have left one, and dependence on a prior legend (the Bennewitz disinformation).
