🇺🇸2023·Colorado Springs, Colorado

United States.The cloud that opened and let out a Mach-2 triangle

In October 2023, an aviation enthusiast working a night shift in Colorado Springs watched a black equilateral triangle, the size of a parking lot, emerge from a stationary, 'pixelated' gray cloud, a red light at each corner. It flew at helicopter speed, then accelerated almost instantly to Mach 1-2, banked sharply in the direction opposite a conventional aircraft, and vanished surrounded by heat-mirage-like waves.

Calling his wife a minute later, she noted his voice sounded 'robotic,' as if there were electronic interference. It's the second FBI/PURSUE file describing a triangle over the same city in the same month.

Primary documents

The night in question

The witness, who said he frequently observes military, commercial, and civilian air traffic over Colorado Springs, was working between 8:00 and 9:30 PM on a night in October 2023. He walked over to a co-worker and both started looking up at the stars on a clear night. Looking up, the witness noticed a very bright star that caught his attention; he watched it for about 5 to 10 seconds before noticing movement in his left peripheral vision. Looking further up and to his left, he saw what appeared to be a dark-gray cloud phasing into view, stationary, with a strange appearance, as if pixelated or distorted in some other way.

As he watched the cloud, he saw a constant, diffuse red light on the bottom-front point of a solid black triangular object emerging from it toward the south-southeast. The part of the object behind the point widened as it exited the cloud, until the witness could see the full equilateral triangle, with a red light at each of its three corners. He estimated the object was enormous (possibly as large as the parking lot he was standing in) and put its thickness at the equivalent of 3-5 stories. At arm's length, each of the three lights was the size of a fingernail.

Initially the object moved south, slightly east, at a steady speed comparable to a helicopter, at an altitude the witness estimated between 3,000 and 5,000 feet, similar to a small civilian aircraft on a training flight. It then accelerated almost instantaneously to what the witness calculated as Mach 1 or 2, turning slightly to head directly south or slightly southwest. He noticed waves surrounding and radiating from the object, which he compared to the mirage effect seen looking down a hot road into the distance. He heard no noise, could see no means of propulsion, and smelled nothing out of the ordinary. As it moved away, the object abruptly banked 90 degrees on its left side, with the three lights still visible, veering to the right, then quickly banked back to level flight; the witness specifically noted that banking motion was opposite to what a conventional aircraft would do turning in that direction. He believed the object passed almost directly over his position, and lost sight of it as it went south over a place whose name is redacted in the FD-302. Looking back to where it had first appeared, the gray cloud had already disappeared.

About 60 seconds later, he called his wife to tell her what happened; she said his voice sounded 'robotic,' as if there were electronic interference on the call. The FBI's formal interview took place in October 2025, two years after the sighting.

Why it matters

This file is, in practice, the second chapter of the same time window as the translucent triangle over Colorado Springs ↗: both describe a large, dark triangle with lights at all three corners and some kind of associated visual distortion (rippling mist in one case, a pixelated cloud and heat mirage in the other) sighted over Colorado Springs during October 2023, by unrelated witnesses. There's no evidence it's the same event (the timing and specific descriptions differ) but the coincidence of city, month, and morphology is a data point worth keeping in view, especially in a city with Colorado Springs's density of military and space-surveillance installations.

What sets this case apart is the specificity of its performance claims: an 'almost instantaneous' acceleration from helicopter speed to 'possibly' Mach 1 or 2 would exceed what a conventional crewed aircraft can tolerate, if the estimate were accurate, though it is a naked-eye estimate, at night, with no known distance to the object; and the banking pattern, tilting in the direction opposite the turn, is aerodynamically counterintuitive for a conventional fixed wing. The witness, who describes himself as a frequent observer of air traffic, anchors those claims in concrete comparisons (helicopter, training aircraft, road mirage), which adds texture but not instrumental verification: these are estimates from a single observer, with no radar or video, and the co-worker who was with him does not appear as a declarant in this file.

The phone call detail (his voice sounding 'robotic' per his wife, immediately after the sighting) is the most relevant element for the patterns: if accurate, it would fit pattern 8k (electromagnetic interference), the same family as Malmstrom or Levelland, though here affecting a cell phone rather than a weapons system or an engine. Neither the FD-302 (which, like all of them, 'contains neither recommendations nor conclusions of the FBI') nor the coverage of the release proposes an explanation for the object, and no element of the account (not the acceleration, not the bank, not the phone interference) has a demonstrated, specific mundane explanation.
