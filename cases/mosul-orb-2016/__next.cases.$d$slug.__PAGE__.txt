16:T434,On April 16, 2016, during coalition operations against the Islamic State, a U.S. reconnaissance aircraft recorded a spherical, metallic-looking object over Mosul, in northern Iraq, according to journalist and documentary filmmaker Jeremy Corbell. According to Corbell and Knapp, the platform was an MC-12, a twin-engine turboprop for medium-to-low-altitude reconnaissance. What is public is a still image that Corbell and George Knapp obtained and released on their Weaponized program in January 2023, and that Corbell shared with Fox News Digital in a February interview that year. Neither the video nor the platform, the sensor or the length of the recording has been released. It is what has since become known as the 'Mosul Orb'. The Pentagon then told The Black Vault it would not comment on remarks by unnamed sources alleging leaks from a classified report, and in June 2023 it denied in full that outlet's FOIA request 23-F-0389 on the case: the records were withheld as classified for national security reasons and as related to an ongoing law enforcement proceeding.17:T506,The case is often linked to the public hearing of the Senate Subcommittee on Emerging Threats and Capabilities on April 19, 2023, but what the director of the All-domain Anomaly Resolution Office (AARO), Sean Kirkpatrick, presented there was a different video: a metallic-looking sphere filmed by an MQ-9 drone in the Middle East in 2022, captured, he specified, with electro-optical sensors, not infrared. Of that video, not the Mosul one, he said 'this is essentially all the data we have of this event' and that 'it's going to be virtually impossible to fully identify that just based off that video'; he presented it as an unresolved case, with 'no demonstration of enigmatic technical capabilities' and consistent with 'other metallic orb observations in the region'. Corbell said afterwards that the admission made him think of the Mosul image. A Fox News photo caption attributes to Kirkpatrick that the Mosul sphere 'is still being studied', but the body of the same article applies that status to the 2022 case.
