14:T47b,The most cited episode took place on 19 August 1970 at Stowell Primary School in Bukit Mertajam, in the state of Penang. According to The Straits Times the next day, six pupils aged 8 to 11 said that, while playing in the scrub beside the school, a soup-plate-sized saucer landed near them and five 'little men' about three inches tall (some 8 centimetres) came down a gangway: the apparent leader wore yellow and the other four blue uniforms; they saw them install an aerial on a tree branch and ran away scared. The youngest, aged 8, said one of the beings took out what looked like a tiny gun and shot him: 'It struck me on my hand, but didn't hurt very much.' The following day other boys reported more: a 10-year-old described one with horns and said that, when he tried to capture it, he was shot, was left with a small red dot on his right leg and fainted, and prefects carried him to a classroom; a 7-year-old came home with a small cut on his hand that, according to his father, a police corporal, one of the beings had made. The headmaster, Ooi Keat Guan, went to the spot with another teacher and found no trace of a ship or the aliens.15:T422,The phenomenon behaved like a wave. The Straits Times kept the tally with some irony: by lunchtime on 21 August 'the score ... was 39 spaceships and 31 spacemen', all seen by Stowell School boys in the nearby scrub. The headmaster questioned the pupils, who stuck to their story, and said he did not believe them: 'They might have seen some leaves or insects moving and mistaken them for tiny spacemen.' CID officers came to question them and thousands of onlookers flocked to the spot, so that the school closed its main gate. Over 90 % of the catalogued witnesses were schoolchildren, and many encounters were placed on school premises. The wave did not end that year: around 1975-76, at the Royal Malaysian Air Force (RMAF) base in Kuantan, a nine-year-old boy said he had seen one of these creatures drinking water from a drain; he described it as brown, «with two feelers on its head, a steel-like rod in its hand and a pistol at its waist», and said he returned that same evening with two friends to see it again.
