2d:T4bb,One night in 2026, two employees of a sensitive federal site in the western United States went out on area-reconnaissance duty. The FBI interviewed them separately: the primary witness in person (FD-302 FBI-UAP-D037) and his colleague by phone (FBI-UAP-D040). According to the primary witness, they left at about 7:45 pm in rain and heavy cloud cover, and between 8:00 and 8:30 pm they saw two unidentified lights, dull red and 'dash-shaped' to the naked eye, moving along the ridgeline of the mountains to the west. They pulled over to watch. Through their Night Optical Devices (NODs) the lights looked 'orb-like' or circular, with the characteristic 'NOD halo' of any light source seen through that equipment; the witness, who has used NODs for more than 25 years, noted it. The statement also records that he has astigmatism: to him the lights looked like dashes, while his companion stated they appeared triangular or diamond-shaped, a difference the document says 'could possibly be attributed' to the astigmatism. After about twenty minutes the two lights disappeared, and shortly afterwards another, similar light appeared directly above their location for about ten minutes before it, too, disappeared.2e:T45a,Sometime between 10:00 and 11:00 pm, still according to the primary witness, another light appeared about 600 meters to the east, apparently about 500 feet above their vehicle, moved toward them and stopped about 400 meters away; a second light approached and stopped near the first, this one about six feet off the ground. As he watched them, he noticed in his peripheral vision a light descend from altitude and stop in a dry wash about 100 meters behind them: he could not see the source directly, only the glow moving through the wash. They drove to the next intersection to get a better view, but the lights were gone, and back at their original position they could not reacquire them. The witness said he was familiar with how stars, satellites and meteors look to the naked eye and through NODs, and that these lights were unlike anything he had seen: they did not track linearly as satellites do, they changed direction and appeared as low as six feet off the ground; nor were they a UAS he was familiar with. Seen up close or while squinting, he said, each light resolved into three lights in a triangle or diamond that 'moved in tandem and never separated, as if all three lights were attached to the same object.' He added that cloud cover was heavy, 'to the point that no stars were visible.' At about 2350 they saw, only through NODs, two lights on or near the ground to the east that stopped some 200-300 meters away, and a rapidly moving streak of light, like a sparkler or flashlight moved quickly, whose glow followed a defilade northward. The two accounts thus differ in time, in the number of lights and in distances. Where they agree is the watch.
