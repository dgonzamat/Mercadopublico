The French analysts confirmed the helicopter flew at a constant 120 knots, that the object was on average about 55 km away, almost exactly the Navy's estimate, and that it kept pace with the helicopter; but they proposed it was a medium-haul aircraft landing at Santiago, with the two hot spots spaced like jet engines and the 'plumes' explained by dumping cabin waste water carried by the west wind.
