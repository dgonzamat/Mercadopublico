2e:T413,In March 1950 Okinawa, in the Ryukyu Islands, was still under United States military administration, with Twentieth Air Force bases and Army anti-aircraft units. At about 10:30 a.m. on March 27, a corporal of the 65th Anti-Aircraft Artillery (AAA) Gun Battalion was operating an SCR-584 "B" radar, serial number 754, which the report says was "in good condition"; the Blue Book record card calls it a "Gun Radar", a radar for aiming anti-aircraft guns. It stood in the gun park at "Kawasake" (so spelled in the document), at 26° 21′ N, 127° 50′ E. The radar picked up an object over the Motobu Peninsula, in the north of the island, at 26° 37′ N, 127° 57′ E, at an altitude of 13,500 feet (the card says 13,000). The report gives a distance to that first point that cannot be read clearly ("1…"), and the card 18 miles; those coordinates give about 20 statute miles to the north-north-east (our own calculation).
