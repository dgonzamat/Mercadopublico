13:T613,The official descriptions accompanying the five videos are notably sparse, and their sparseness is deliberate. None asserts that there is an object. The term they use is 'area of contrast', a formula describing what the sensor registers —a region of the thermal image differing from its surroundings— without committing to anything physical producing it. PR143 is exhausted in one line: across its nineteen seconds the sensor pans to track the area of contrast, keeping it generally centred. PR144 records that for the first two seconds the area is visible above and to the left of the centre of the frame, and that from the third the sensor increases its magnification and tracks it. PR150 records that the area enters the field of view from the bottom edge, that the sensor tracks it, and that between seconds fifty-two and fifty-seven the platform's own geometry occludes the view, blurring the image and blocking the right side of the screen. PR151 notes that at sixteen seconds the sensor increases its level of magnification and continues tracking. PR152, the longest, is likewise summarised in a single line: the sensor pans to track the area of contrast, keeping it generally centred, for one minute and thirty-eight seconds. All five close with the same disclaimer, which is worth taking seriously: the description is offered for informational purposes only and the reader should not interpret any part of it as an analytical judgment, an investigative conclusion or a factual determination about the event's validity, nature or significance.14:T4b7,One sentence in PR150's record deserves attention, because read literally it says the opposite of what its own construction implies. Describing the rectangular overlay that appears near the centre of the frame, the published text reads: 'This display element is a graphical component of the sensor system and does indicate a physical change in the environment.' The sentence presents the overlay as a graphical component of the sensor system —that is, an interface artefact— and then states that it does indicate a physical change in the environment. The two halves contradict each other. The correct reading was confirmed by checking the rest of the release: the same sentence, identical character for character, also appears in the record for DOW-UAP-PR133 on the Middle East, a file unrelated to this one. Its recurrence across two independent items proves it is not one analyst's slip in drafting but boilerplate text, and that what the boilerplate meant to say is that the overlay does not indicate any physical change: it is the focus marker the system itself draws.
