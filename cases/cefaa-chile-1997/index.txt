27:T423,Under Bermúdez's leadership the committee reached its greatest international visibility. Chilean Army captain and pilot Rodrigo Bravo Garrido, named the Army's representative to the committee in 2009, brought the body an aeronautical-methodological approach and an explicit interest in the formal study of the phenomenon.
