16:T929,Unlike the USS Omaha 'transmedium' sphere, the Russell 'pyramid' clip has a strong and widely accepted conventional explanation. Skeptical analyst Mick West showed that the triangular shape is an optical defocus artifact (bokeh): an out-of-focus point light source (an aircraft, a star or a drone) takes on the shape of the camera's aperture, and many photographic lenses have bladed apertures that produce triangular or polygonal bokeh. The flashing fits aircraft navigation lights and the slow drift fits distant objects. The official explanation came on May 17, 2022, when Deputy Director of Naval Intelligence Scott Bray showed this same video before the House Intelligence Counterterrorism, Counterintelligence, and Counterproliferation Subcommittee, at Congress's first public UAP hearing in over 50 years, and it is more precise on one point: according to Bray, this video, recorded while the ship observed a number of small unmanned aerial systems, 'remained unresolved for several years'; what resolved it was a later case, off a different coast, in which Navy personnel, again in a swarm of drones and again through night-vision goggles and an SLR camera, recorded the same image while other Navy assets observed drones nearby. 'We are now reasonably confident that these triangles correlate to unmanned aerial systems in the area', he said, attributing the triangular appearance to 'light passing through the night vision goggles and then being recorded by an SLR camera'. Asked by Adam Schiff, he said the UAP Task Force was aware of studies that had reproduced the effect by flying a drone. Per the official description accompanying the footage's later public release, the objects (there described as 'multiple objects... harassing Navy ships participating in Naval Exercises off Southern California') were 'subsequently reclassified as unmanned aerial systems' based on additional information from other sightings. That is more categorical language than Bray's own at the hearing ('reasonably confident'), which rested the reclassification on the analogy with the later case; the DVIDS record, moreover, does not name the ship: it says the video was 'taken by Sailors onboard one of the ships'.
