29:T432,On February 12, 2023, a U.S. F-16 shot down an object flying at about 20,000 feet over Lake Huron, near the Michigan-Ontario border. The order came from President Joe Biden. A senior administration official described it to the press as an octagonal structure with strings hanging off and no discernible payload. According to the Pentagon, the first AIM-9X missile missed and the second hit. In the Department of Defense statement issued that Sunday, which the press attributed to its spokesman, Brig. Gen. Pat Ryder, the Pentagon said that NORAD 'detected the object Sunday morning and has maintained visual and radar tracking of it' and that, 'based on its flight path and data', it could reasonably connect it to the radar signal picked up the day before over Montana, which had flown in proximity to sensitive Department sites.
