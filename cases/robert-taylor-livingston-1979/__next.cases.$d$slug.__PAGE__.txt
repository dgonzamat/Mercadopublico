Taylor's personal credibility was a central factor: those who knew him described him as an honest man not given to fantasy —his employer, Malcolm Drummond, stated flatly that he was not one to make up stories—, he did not drink, and he held to the same account without changing it until his death in 2007. Prosaic explanations point to a medical episode —Taylor suffered from epilepsy, and a seizure, mini-stroke or episode producing olfactory hallucination and a fall has been suggested— while the ground marks never received an agreed explanation (forestry machinery was floated, unconfirmed).
