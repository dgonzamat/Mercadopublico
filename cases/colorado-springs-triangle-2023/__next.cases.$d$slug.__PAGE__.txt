18:T485,The triangle moved in a straight line at a constant altitude, covering a large distance in very little time. Just before he lost sight of it, the left side banked sharply to a near-vertical orientation, without changing course or altitude. It was completely silent: the witness felt no vibration, static, or any other effect as it passed overhead. He lived near an airport and was used to seeing and hearing private planes and helicopters fly over his house regularly; he also said he was well acquainted with USAF aircraft, including the newest unmanned drones, and that the object matched nothing he'd encountered before: it was only a triangle, with no wings, fins or bubble for an engine housing. The entire observation lasted about four seconds, from when he first noticed it to when it vanished to the southwest (sooner than he expected) with no time to pull out his phone to record it. His wife additionally recalled a dark seam running along the object's edge. Both went inside immediately, into separate rooms; what they did there is redacted in the FD-302.
