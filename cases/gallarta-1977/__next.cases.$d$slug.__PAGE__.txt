1a:T57b,The investigating officer, a captain and aeronautical technical engineer from Industry Territorial Zone No. 4 appointed by the Bilbao Air Commander, asked Benítez for the addresses of the witnesses, who did not wish to be identified. According to the file's summary he found two, who 'turned out to be father-in-law and son-in-law'. The son-in-law, a naval engineer and technical director of a company in Santurce, stated that on 13 February, at a distance he put at one kilometre, he saw 'an artefact rise, almost vertically and at enormous speed, which without making any noise at all disappeared into the sky'. He described it as shaped like 'a flattened mushroom', with a dome on one face and a kind of legs on the other, steel grey and surrounded by a very bright halo 'which could well have been a reflection of the sun'; he estimated it at 20 to 25 metres across and the sighting at one to two seconds, and said it left in the air a kind of thread that vanished in fractions of a second. The time does not match across the papers: his statement says 'around 4 in the afternoon', the form records 14:00 Z and the Air Operations Command summary 'around 1700 local'. The officer wrote that the witness 'seems to offer every guarantee for believing his statements'; asked about the neighbour who, according to the report, had seen UFOs four times, the engineer replied that it was his father-in-law.1b:T5ad,The father-in-law, a 50-year-old formwork carpenter living in the La Florida district, described in an interview of some two and a half hours five appearances between mid-February and 29 April, the last one after the press report. In the first, at around two in the morning, he heard a call with his name, went out, crossed a small wood and saw on the open ground a saucer that rocked and pushed out and drew in some legs; a sliding door opened and two beings in overalls came down, told him not to be afraid and said they had to leave because they had been detected. In the second, two other beings told him they had come to help the Earth because it 'was leaving its orbit and was tilting too much'. In the third a small, almost cubic 'tankette', one metre a side, came down from the saucer and seemed to take soil samples; he touched the craft, whose metal surface had a kind of welded scales, and shook hands with one of the beings, whose hand was 'terribly cold'. On 29 April, at around 11 at night, while he was watching the programme 'Un, dos, tres', the saucer landed some 35 metres from his house and three beings shook his hand. The officer noted that the open ground held 'countless marks' that confirmed nothing, because the rain and the hundreds of sightseers who came after the report had churned everything up; only a few, which he drew, could form an arc of a circle 40 to 50 metres across.
