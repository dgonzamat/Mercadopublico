11:T444,According to the witnesses' consistent account, Hernández, who was driving, noticed a fixed orange light in the distance, first attributed to a nitrate-works train or an illuminated building. As they advanced, the light stayed aligned with the vehicle: it switched off and on as the car stopped and started, and over time closed to an estimated distance of 800 meters to a kilometer. The passengers then saw a second, smaller orange light appear on the sand beside the road; when the car stopped a second time, both lights turned white. Shortly after, the group crossed paths with a truck; they signaled it to switch from high to low beams, and the truck driver — not yet knowing what was happening — did the same. At that moment, according to the account, the light on the road grew in intensity and began emitting colored flashes — orange, blue, violet — before merging with the light on the sand into a single object.
