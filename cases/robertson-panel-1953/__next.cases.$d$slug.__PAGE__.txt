The panel did not accept that conclusion: after viewing a film of seagulls reflecting sunlight, it considered that the objects strongly suggested birds, and attributed the Great Falls objects to reflections from aircraft known to be in the area. It concluded that there was no indication of a direct physical threat, no residuum of cases attributable to foreign artifacts capable of hostile acts, and nothing requiring a revision of current scientific concepts. 1c:T468,The exact scope of the evidence should be stated precisely, without over-reading it. The report does not claim UFOs are craft of non-human origin or that the government recovered any; what it prescribes is the management of public perception for Cold War counterintelligence reasons. A legitimate skeptical reading holds that the panel acted in good faith on a real problem — the saturation of early-warning channels — and that 'debunking' was a security measure, not a cover-up of substantive knowledge.
