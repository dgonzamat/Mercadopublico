1a:T53a,There is, however, a methodological resonance that makes that boundary more porous than SETI would like. The standard the 2026 protocol raises to a norm —independent multi-instrument verification, cross-confirmation by separate institutions, a ban on announcing without replication— is exactly the one the corpus uses to qualify a case as Tier S. The yardstick by which SETI would measure an extraterrestrial radio signal is the one that, applied honestly, would validate the best UAP cases SETI refuses to look at.
