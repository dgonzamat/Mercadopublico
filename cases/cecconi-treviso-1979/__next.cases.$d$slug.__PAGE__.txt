16:T956,The official record from the time that is public today is minimal. The 1972–1990 sighting catalogue that the Aeronautica Militare published in 2023 devotes a single entry to the case: Treviso, 18/06/1979, «morning, not specified», shape «cylindrical», colour «black», speed and direction «not indicated», altitude «about 3,000 m», clear sky, reported by Aeronautica Militare personnel. Everything else comes from Cecconi's testimony, first gathered by ufologist Antonio Chiumiento of the Centro Ufologico Nazionale and published in Flying Saucer Review in 1988, and in interviews in 1994 and 1995. The most detailed reconstruction is Marco Orlandi's for the CISU's AIRCAT project, based on in-person interviews with Cecconi in September 1994 and a telephone conversation on 2 August 1995. According to it, Cecconi switched on all four cameras and flew past the object at about 300 knots, as close as 70 to 80 metres: he made seven or eight passes and collected 82 frames. When he began, the object was at about 7,000 feet (some 2,100 metres); it then climbed and descended in steps of about a thousand feet until it reached roughly 13,000 (some 3,900 metres). Relative to his passes it seemed practically motionless, but the Istrana radar centre told him it was in fact moving «on a definite course and at a definite speed», and the pilot had the impression that it turned so as never to show him its side. The Treviso tower, whose staff were following it through binoculars, radioed that a «bluish trail» was coming from the object, which he, although so close, did not see. He described it as a dull black «fuel tank», about 8 metres long and 2.5 or at most 3 in diameter, with a kind of whitish «cupoletta», more a fairing than a dome, on its slightly flattened top; other versions, such as the 1995 interview, give about five metres by one and a half. He insisted that it did not shake as the jet passed beside it and that it «was not plastic and did not look like an inflatable balloon». As he turned for another pass, Istrana told him the return had vanished from the screen between two sweeps, and a few seconds later the tower reported it could no longer see it. 22:T64c,The prosaic reading is serious, though less settled than the 1984 letter suggests. In 1979 «UFO solari» were sold in Italy: toy balloons shaped like a black plastic tube several metres long, filled with air, which rise when the sun warms them.
