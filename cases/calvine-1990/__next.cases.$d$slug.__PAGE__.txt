15:T48c,On the evening of 4 August 1990, two men —by the account, two cooks from a nearby hotel walking on the moors above the hamlet of Calvine, in Perthshire (Scotland)— observed a large diamond-shaped object hovering silently in the sky. They took shelter under some trees and from there shot a series of six photographs; according to the witnesses, the object hovered silently for four or five minutes and then slowly ascended vertically, gradually gaining speed. In at least four of the six images, alongside the diamond-shaped object, appears what has been interpreted as a military jet —probably a Harrier— flying nearby, a decisive detail because it suggests military aircraft were present in the scene. According to the MoD file quoted by Clarke (DEFE 24/1940/1), its specialists established that the jet was a Harrier and identified "a barely visible second aircraft, again probably a Harrier"; yet, according to the ministry's own briefing, none were operating in Scotland at that date and time. In 2022 Lindsay showed him that material and the image was made public for the first time.
