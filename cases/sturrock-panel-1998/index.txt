2b:T477,Its importance is methodological and legitimizing, not probative. The document's value to the corpus is not what it says about the phenomenon —it says little, and cautiously— but what it says about how the subject should be treated: as an open empirical problem whose resolution runs through better instruments and better data, not through a priori discredit. It anticipates by a quarter century the framework NASA would adopt in 2023 and the logic of the corpus itself: weigh the evidence, do not proclaim conclusions. Its limit is evident and the panel accepts it: it reviewed pre-existing evidence of heterogeneous quality, without collecting new data, and its product is a recommendation of method, not a finding. That is why its weight is minimal and operates only by slightly eroding the mundane/natural narrative —the reading that the whole phenomenon reduces to misidentification—, because qualified scientists, looking at the set, could not dismiss the entire physical residue.
