28:T52c,What sets the case apart is that it was set down in an operational document. That same day the tower transmitted an Accident and/or Incident Report (RAI) over the aeronautical network, which CORPAC's AIS-ARO team —the Corporación Peruana de Aeropuertos y Aviación Comercial, the state company that provides air traffic control— stamped as received on 27 February. Its description field says that from the tower 'apparently two UFOs' are observed 'west of the station approx 8000FT', that one of them 'appears intermittently on the radar screen at 5NM RDL274' (five nautical miles on the 274 radial, almost due west) and that the area control center supervisor was notified; the additional-information field notes that at 07:12 UTC the objects were no longer in view of the tower.
