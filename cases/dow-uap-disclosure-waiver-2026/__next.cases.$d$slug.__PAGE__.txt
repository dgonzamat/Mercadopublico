13:T56a,On 31 July 2026, Aaron Lukas, the Principal Deputy Director of National Intelligence, signed memorandum ES 2026-00818, 'Preliminary Guidance for Implementation of NDA Waiver', distributed to eighteen recipients across departments and agencies. Its operative sentence leaves no doubt about the scope: 'Consistent with Presidential Direction, the Department of War (DoW) and the Intelligence Community (IC) will implement a process' so that current and former employees and contractors with relevant UAP information 'shall have prior non-disclosure agreements (NDA), agreements, oaths, or commitments waived' when speaking with authorized representatives of AARO or PURSUE. It does not touch criminal liability either, nor does it authorise public disclosure.
