26:T4fc,The wave began in November 1966 around Point Pleasant, a small West Virginia town on the banks of the Ohio River. According to reports gathered by the local press and county police, several witnesses described a grey winged figure as tall as a man —«about six feet tall», in Mary Hyre's first report—, with large widely-spaced red eyes and a considerable wingspan, able to rise almost vertically and to follow a car at high speed. According to John Keel, who interviewed the witnesses from December 1966 and gathered 26 sightings between November 1966 and November 1967, ten of them by the «TNT Area», all agreed that it did not flap its wings; one of them, Thomas Ury, described it on 25 November as about six feet long with an eight-to-ten-foot wingspan.
