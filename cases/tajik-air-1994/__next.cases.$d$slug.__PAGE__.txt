1e:T606,The case illustrates a virtue and a limit of the diplomatic record at once. The virtue: that three foreign professional pilots went to a U.S. embassy and that the State Department drafted, dated and kept the report for three decades gives the episode an institutional traceability unusual for a sighting from the region. The limit: the cable faithfully conveys what the pilots said, not what an instrument measured, and the photographs that might have elevated the case never appeared in the file. A prolonged visual observation at high altitude also admits conventional explanations —a bright planet or star whose apparent motion induces an illusion of maneuvering (autokinesis), reflections, other air traffic or re-entries— that the available material cannot rule out. That is why the case is testimonially strong and documentarily solid yet stays at mid-tier: exemplary chain of custody, physical corroboration absent.
