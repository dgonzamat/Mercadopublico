The Institute of Geological Sciences (now the British Geological Survey) recorded a magnitude 3.5 earthquake that night with its epicentre in the region, felt across a wide area of north Wales and as far as Formby, in England, about 20 km north of Liverpool. Separately, numerous observers in Wales and northern England reported that night a bright meteor (a bolide) crossing the sky; the press placed it at the time of the explosion, but the investigation by the British UFO Research Association (BUFORA) published in spring 1974 timed it at about 9:58 p.m., an hour and twenty minutes after the tremor, at magnitude −7, and concluded that the two were unconnected events the press had lumped together. An earthquake explains the roar and the vibration; a bolide explains the moving light.
