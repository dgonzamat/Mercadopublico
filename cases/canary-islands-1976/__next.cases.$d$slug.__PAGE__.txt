In a taxi heading towards La Rosa, the driver and a doctor on a house call said they saw a sphere some 30 metres across, with two red figures inside, stationary very close to the ground at 15 to 20 metres; the third occupant did not see it because the car roof blocked his view. At Boca-Barranco, on the bay of Punta Gáldar, a couple saw a light like a car headlight that grew to three times the lunar disc and faded, leaving a blue arc. At Agaete, a teacher saw a great white circle, so transparent that the stars showed through it. A taxi driver climbing Gáldar mountain, a municipal policeman, a farmer, a labourer and a woman whose television went off described a great luminous circle or globe with a halo, and the woman thought she saw two figures inside. At 21:45 a sergeant on duty at Pozo de las Nieves saw a circular object the size of three full moons appear from behind Tenerife, rise to about 45° and vanish after ten minutes.16:T580,On 1 July 1976, prompted by press reports, the Chief of the Air Staff asked the Canary Islands Air Zone by cipher telegram for detailed information; the zone had already appointed an investigating judge on 25 June. Report 01/76 was sent to Madrid on 21 July. It includes traffic data from the Las Palmas Control Centre and a report from radar station EVA No. 8, whose commander stated that no anomalous tracks were seen and the equipment showed no faults; meteorological information; the statements of 13 witnesses, graded by reliability (three military men and sailors in the highest category of the group); the judge's analysis; and an interview and medical examination of one of the witnesses by a physician. The general commanding the Air Zone endorsed the judge: 'It is a phenomenon observed on the islands of Fuerteventura, Gran Canaria and Tenerife by an appreciable number of reliable persons who agree on the fundamental points, and not detected by radar. It has not been possible to determine either its origin or its nature, so it must be given the status of an "Unidentified Aerial Phenomenon".' The judge spoke to the Diario de Las Palmas in May 1977, and that year J. J. Benítez published material from the report.
