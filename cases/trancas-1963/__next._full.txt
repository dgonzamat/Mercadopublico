28:T82a,On the night of 21 October 1963, at a Moreno family farm in the town of Trancas, Tucumán province (Argentina), the residents —the elderly couple Antonio and Teresa Moreno, three adult daughters, the children of one of them and the domestic worker; the husbands of two of the daughters, army officers, were away— reported the appearance of luminous objects. The chain of events, according to the accounts, began when a teenage domestic worker insisted she saw lights over the embankment of the Belgrano railway, in front of the house, some 200 meters away by some accounts and about 300 yards by Creighton's compilation. The witnesses described several objects —the accounts speak of up to six— one of which descended and settled near the home, by the tracks, where it stayed for about 45 minutes; others lined up in the distance. The people described a beam of light projected toward the house, a penetrating smell, and a sensation of heat and confinement as they sheltered inside. The wire report the Corriere della Sera published on 25 October already summed up the accounts this way: six discs about eight metres across, hovering some 40 minutes a few tens of metres up, with a smell of sulphur, a rise in temperature and two beams, one white and one purplish-red; and it added that the witnesses were «seemingly victims of a collective hallucination». According to Creighton's compilation, the maid and one of the daughters, Julia Moreno de Colotti, saw by the track «something like a small train, intensely illuminated» with figures moving about it, and Antonio Moreno said he saw silhouettes at the portholes of one disc. Gordon Creighton's press compilation also records that some neighbours, José Acosta and the Huanca family, saw the illumination on the embankment from a distance, and that Francisco Tropiano, some two kilometres away, saw six discs cross the sky between 10:15 and 10:20 p.m., the time the siege of the house ended.
