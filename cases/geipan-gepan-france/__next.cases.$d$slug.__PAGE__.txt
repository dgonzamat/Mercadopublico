14:T468,Its reference work is the investigation of Trans-en-Provence (8 January 1981), considered one of the best-documented 'physical trace' cases in history. A resident, Renato Nicolaï, reported hearing a whistling sound and seeing a disc-shaped object briefly land on his property. Alerted immediately, the gendarmerie preserved the site, and GEPAN commissioned laboratory analyses of the soil and vegetation: the results indicated the ground had been compressed by a mechanical pressure on the order of several tons and heated to an estimated temperature between 300 and 600 °C, along with biochemical alterations in the nearby plants that were not trivially explained. The case is still debated —not even GEIPAN itself presents it as proof of exotic origin—, but it exemplifies the method: rapid collection, chain of custody, third-party analysis and publication.
