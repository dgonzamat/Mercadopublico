2f:T460,On the night of February 8, 2005, a military radar controller at Grazzanise, in the province of Caserta, logged two slow contacts over the air base that the Italian Air Force could not match to any known flight. The press describes the base as one of the most important in Italy. The event was recorded in the annual register that the Italian Air Force publishes on its institutional website, in the table headed "Avvistamenti O.V.N.I. – Febbraio 2005". The table holds two entries for that night, both at Grazzanise: the first at 23:35 local time and the second at 23:45. In both, the source of the report is a "controllore radar militare" (military radar controller). The shape and color are given as "non specificata" and "non specificato" (not specified), the speed as "ridotta" (reduced) and the direction of motion as "variabile" (variable). Weather conditions are not specified either. The recorded height is "3.000 ft circa" (about 900 meters) in the first entry and "compresa tra 1.000 e 3.000 ft" (between 300 and 900 meters) in the second.
