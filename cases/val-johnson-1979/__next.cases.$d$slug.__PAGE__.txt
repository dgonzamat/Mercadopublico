No single prosaic cause accounts cleanly for all of those at once, which is precisely the difficulty Allan Hendry named. Yet the same convergence can be read the other way: a constellation of effects with no agreed mechanism and a sole witness is also the signature of a poorly understood or staged event, and skeptics have stressed that the physical damage, examined on its own, does not point unambiguously to an external impact.
