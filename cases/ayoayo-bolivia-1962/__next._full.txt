The Secretary of State also received it as a NIACT, the flag that requires action at once even at night: "ACTION SECSTATE NIACT 20, JULY 7, 6 PM". The subject was the "sighting of missile or rocket approximately 35 nautical miles south of La Paz and eight nautical miles west of Ayoayo", some 65 kilometres south of La Paz and 15 west of the town. The message ends by announcing that the search would continue "with larger ground party and available conventional aircraft". It is signed "FOX", who was then the air attaché in Bolivia, Lieutenant Colonel Edward J.
