The pilots' size and speed estimates implied extreme dynamics, with speeds on the order of thousands of kilometers per hour and an acute-angle turn. The incident occurred about four months after Project Blue Book received that name and five days before the first radar events over Washington. The next morning, in Miami, agents of the Air Force Office of Special Investigations (OSI) interrogated the pilots; according to Nash, they were told the investigators already had seven other reports of red discs in formation, none of which appear in the Blue Book file. The report by Major John H. Sharpe (AF Form 112, 15 July) records among the two pilots' impressions that the objects "are definitely not of earthly origin", and reckons they were in view for at least five seconds, possibly ten. The Blue Book record card summarizes the case: six disc-shaped objects, 100 ft in diameter, emitting a reddish glow, approached the airliner at about 2,000 ft, made an erratic turn to avoid collision and sped away in their original formation.
