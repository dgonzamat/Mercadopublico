11:T539,Indonesia's record in the corpus is not an isolated sighting but a pattern of institutional engagement spanning generations of its Air Force. The founding figure is Raden Jacob Salatun (1927–2012), an air marshal (three-star officer) of the TNI-AU, the Indonesian Air Force. Salatun was a central figure of the national aerospace program: he founded the Astronautics Committee in 1962, was one of the creators in 1963 of the national institute of aeronautics and space (LAPAN), which he headed from 1971 to 1978, and held a ministerial post in Sukarno's government (linked to the aviation industry). From that position of technical and political authority, he was the pioneer of UFO study in Indonesia, approaching it explicitly from an aerospace, scientific and national-security angle. In a 1974 text he set out his thesis: that the UFO problem had to be studied seriously for sociological, technological and security reasons.
