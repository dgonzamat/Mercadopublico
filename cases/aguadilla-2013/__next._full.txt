2c:T451,A researcher connected to the team received a copy of the original file on 20 or 21 October 2013 from an acquaintance of the crew. The SCU report, signed by Robert Powell, Morgan Beall, Daina Chaviano, Larry Cates, Carl Paulson and Richard Hoffman and dated 2015, split the video into 7,027 frames and estimated a size of three to five feet, airborne speeds of 40 to 120 mph (median about 80 mph) and an average of 82.8 mph under water with no appreciable deceleration on entry. Using FAA radar data obtained under FOIA from the Air Force's 84th RADES squadron, it validated the aircraft's track and found returns without transponders over the sea two to three miles north and northwest of the airport. Its original report also holds that the object briefly disappears behind a tree, which would put it below 40 feet, and the transponder-less radar returns north of the airport have not been explained by anyone. But the weight of evidence has shifted: two independent reconstructions, one using the aircraft's geometry, agree on passive objects drifting with the wind, and a pinkish light that goes out as it nears the coast is what one would expect of a lantern.
