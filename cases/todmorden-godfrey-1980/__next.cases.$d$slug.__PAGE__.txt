Godfrey tried to radio on VHF and UHF and got nothing, though he himself pointed out that there are many radio blank spots in the area. He took out his notebook and drew a sketch of the object as he watched it. Then —and this is the critical point of the case— he reported a discontinuity: in an instant the object was no longer there and he found himself driving farther along the road, with no memory of the transition. According to the 1981 report, he then heard a voice —he thinks inside his head— saying «You should not be seeing this. It is not for your eyes, Davies. You will forget it» (Davies is the report's pseudonym); he was no longer sure what, if anything, he had forgotten. The car was in first gear and moving slowly. He drove back toward town, picked up a colleague and found it was 5:30: his conscious memory covered about four minutes since he had left the station at 5:15, so about ten were missing.
