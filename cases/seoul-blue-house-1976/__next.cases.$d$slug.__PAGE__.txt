15:T80a,The trajectory carried the formation toward the restricted airspace protecting the Blue House (Cheong Wa Dae), the residence and office of the president, then Park Chung-hee, one of the strictest exclusion zones in the country. In 1976 Seoul's defenses were at maximum tension: barely two months earlier, the Panmunjom axe murder incident had brought the peninsula to the brink of war, and the anti-aircraft systems ringing the capital were primed to answer any intrusion from the north. According to those witnesses, when the group of lights penetrated the restricted zone, several batteries opened fire. The report the Korean government passed to Washington, which the State Department repeated that night to callers (telegram STATE 255220), distinguishes two shootings: at 18:13 Northwest cargo flight 902, just after taking off from Kimpo, entered the restricted area at 8,000 feet and the capital security forces fired over 1,000 rounds at it without hitting it; at 18:32 they fired about 700 more at "a suspected unidentified propeller driven aircraft"; the same text says no US aircraft were involved in that shooting. The Ministry of Defense statement the next day speaks only of warning fire around 18:19 at an unknown aircraft, later identified as Boeing 707 number 902, and does not mention the second target. The ordnance that went up came back down over the neighborhoods, including the US embassy compound and the UN Command residence areas. On October 15 Vice Minister of Defense Yi Min-u told the National Assembly defense committee that 21 people had been injured, 8 seriously, and the embassy noted that no deaths had been reported so far (telegram SEOUL 8287). On October 22 the embassy reported that the Seoul press now gave the death of one of the injured and a total of 32 injured, and that the Seoul city government would pay compensation for the deceased and medical expenses for the others (telegram SEOUL 8478). The casualties were caused by the military response itself, and the ministry expressed regret and promised compensation.16:T82a,The explanation offered at the time was that the 'Blue House UFO' had been a U.S. cargo plane that had strayed into the exclusion zone. The ministry attributed the incident to a chartered Northwest Airlines Boeing 707 freighter that had taken off from Gimpo for Osaka and violated prohibited zone P-73 through a navigational error; the State Department telegram gives Tokyo as the destination and says the plane landed there without being hit and that the crew stated they were not aware they had been fired upon; asked whether the plane was inside the area, the Department answered that it could not confirm this precisely at that time. The ministry's investigation, as informally reported to the UN Command, found that Kimpo air traffic control had given the plane an incorrect routing, corrected only when it had neared, or possibly entered, the prohibited airspace. The embassy added that this did not relieve the pilot, since visibility was adequate to see the high-intensity strobe lights surrounding the city. The control records SBS showed in 2020 point the same way: around 18:04 the controller warned it that it might enter the zone, the plane entered three minutes later and kept its course for about 30 minutes. But witnesses described a dozen lights in formation, and the programme judged it hard to identify them with that plane; the telegrams say nothing of lights in formation, and the 18:32 target remains unidentified in them. That night, moreover, presenter Lee Soo-man described the sighting live on MBC's radio show 'Byeoli bichnaneun bame'.
