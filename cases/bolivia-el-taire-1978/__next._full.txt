29:T671,On 18 May, the State Department replied in a secret cable that, after checking with the appropriate agencies, "no direct correlation with known space objects that may have reentered the earth's atmosphere near May 6 can be made", though they were continuing to examine possibilities, and asked for specific data on the object's trajectory, time and markings.
