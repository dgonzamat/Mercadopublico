14:T4e5,Mexican pilot and investigator Alejandro Franz Navarrete, of the Alcione organization, advanced the most influential and, over time, dominant alternative explanation: the FLIR may have detected the gas flares of the oil platforms in the Cantarell field, in the Campeche sound, one of the most intense extraction zones in the Gulf of Mexico, located between 50 and 90 km from Ciudad del Carmen. At that distance and near the horizon, fixed heat sources can appear as discrete objects suspended at the aircraft's altitude, especially if a thermal inversion refracts their infrared signature. Franz strengthened his argument by consulting NASA's FIRMS system, which confirmed the presence of active heat sources in the Cantarell area on that very March 5, 2004 —that is, the platforms were indeed flaring gas that day—. The hypothesis is consistent with the geography (flight over the Gulf), with the total absence of visual detection, and with the fact that the 'objects' never actually closed in. The FAM has issued no updated official conclusion confirming or refuting it.
