16:T68e,The account became public through the press: J. J. Benítez published it in the magazine Mundo Desconocido (no. 17, November 1977), and in 1981 the Diario de Caracas reprinted it. The file the Ministry of Defense declassified in 1996 (no. 761112, Air Operations Command, 28 pages, now in the Virtual Library of Defense) grows from there, not from a base report: it is opened by the defense attaché in Caracas, who in April 1982 forwards the article in case the Intelligence Section had not seen it, and it is completed by letters from private citizens asking for information. The official answers say little. In 1984 the Air Staff's second-in-command sets the reply to a citizen: to establish the cause of the events, the judicial authority 'appointed an Examining Judge' who sent it the summary information, 'there being no record of the event at the Air Staff', and all information on UFOs remained classified; in 1985 the Defense Information and Social Relations Directorate (DRISDE) repeats to another citizen that, to clarify what happened, 'an Examining Judge was appointed, who forwarded the relevant conclusions to the Judicial Authority'; in 1991 the acting commander of Wing 23 writes that no document on the matter exists, and the Air Operations Command's enquiries to the base and to DRISDE found neither that report nor any other. The case sheet the Command itself drew up on declassification marks 'file reliability: missing!' and summarizes the event from the press feature.
