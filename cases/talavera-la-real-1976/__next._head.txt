5:T42d,In the early hours of 12 November 1976, three conscript soldiers on guard in the fuel area of Talavera la Real Air Base (Badajoz, Spain) reported interference, a high-pitched whistle, a partial blackout and an agitated military dog. One of them, José Manuel Trejo, described a humanoid figure some three meters tall formed by points of green light, with long arms and no hands or feet, before losing consciousness; his two companions opened fire on the apparition, which vanished, and the next day neither casings nor impacts were found. The file the Ministry of Defense declassified in 1996 contains no report of the incident: it gathers the press feature and the official correspondence, in which the Air Staff says it has no 'record of the event', although a 1985 letter states that an examining judge was appointed and forwarded his conclusions to the judicial authority, a report that never turned up.
